# CLAUDE.md — Šank

Internal order + shift + bar-stock + owner-dashboard app for one shisha lounge in Bosnia and Herzegovina. The master plan (data model, flows, rules, roadmap, open decisions) lives in **PLAN.md** — read it before any task. Research appendices are in `docs/research/`, the review history in `docs/reviews.md`.

## Owner context

- Vedran is a **junior web developer trained only on Vue 3 / Nuxt** — explain non-Vue concepts (transactions, triggers, WAL, service workers, ETag, scrypt, moving average) in a two-line comment or README paragraph where they first appear; never assume backend/devops experience beyond what `~/Projects/snajper` already ships.
- Model split: **Fable plans and reviews, Opus 5 implements** via subagents, one PR per roadmap bullet or coherent slice. After each phase: `nuxt build` + typecheck + vitest + a visual check on a phone-sized viewport (real phone for offline work), then commit.
- The café owner is Vedran's friend. One venue now; `venue_id` on every table so a second café is an insert, not a rewrite.

## Stack & conventions

- Nuxt 4 (Vue 3 + TypeScript, `<script setup>`), Tailwind CSS v4, Pinia + VueUse, Drizzle ORM + better-sqlite3 (WAL), Nitro server routes, Zod schemas in `shared/`, vitest. Same folder shape as snajper: `app/`, `server/`, `shared/`, `deploy/`, `tests/`.
- Two layouts: `/konobar` — waiter app (dark theme, mobile-first, installable PWA, offline outbox with client UUIDs); `/admin` — owner dashboard (phone + laptop).
- UI language Bosnian (ijekavian). Money is integer feninga in `*_fen` columns, formatted `1.250,50 KM`; dates `08.09.2026.`; 24 h clock; the business day starts 06:00 `Europe/Sarajevo` — no business code calls `getHours()` without a zone (`shared/dates.ts`).
- Ledger tables are append-only, enforced by SQLite triggers in `server/database/triggers.sql`, re-applied at boot after `migrate()`. The invariant tests are the guardrail: never weaken one to fit a feature; a test change needs a sentence in the PR naming the invariant that moved. `tests/unit/invariants.test.ts` holds the four cross-package identities (the summary reconciling three ways, `venueExpected = drawer + Σ waiters`, *Puls* against the ledger, *Kategorije* against the written summaries).
- **Two roles: `admin | radnik`.** `'owner'`, `'waiter'` and `'bartender'` are not accepted values anywhere after `0005_radnik.sql` — the owner *dashboard* is `/admin` and the role guarding it is `admin`; `radnik` is everybody else. Which staff screen a worker is on tonight is **not** a property of his account: it is a `ScreenMode` (`konobar | sanker`) on his **session**, chosen after the PIN and switchable without signing out, and `shared/landing.ts` is the one place that turns role + mode + shift into a destination. The one thing that was ever not identical between a waiter and a bartender — the bartender being a default approver — was always `settings.approver_roles` and never a role rule; it now reads `['admin','radnik']`, so that setting is the only thing standing between a worker and somebody else's money. Authorization is deny-by-default through one table, `shared/routeRoles.ts`: `server/middleware/tenant.ts` normalises `method + path` and **403s when the key is absent**, so a route nobody declared is dead rather than open. `tests/unit/route-roles.test.ts` asserts `ROUTE_ROLES` and `server/api/**` are the same set, both directions.
- **Two shifts a day, and they overlap.** The café runs *Prva smjena* 07–15 and *Druga smjena* 15–23 (`shift_templates`), and the evening crew starts serving before the morning crew's last guests have paid — the quarter of an hour every handover takes. So **two `shifts` rows can be open at once**: `shifts_one_open_uq` is one open shift per business date *per `template_id`*, never one per venue. A shift is born when a worker picks it on *Koju smjenu radiš?* (`POST /api/auth/shift`), the step after the screen, and carries the slot he named — nothing infers it from the clock, because a crew starting at 14:50 opens inside the morning's window and used to be drawn as a *Vanredna smjena* the café does not have. A slot is free **per screen**: the konobar seat and the šank seat of one shift are taken separately, and a seat is read off sessions (`sessions.shift_id` + `sessions.mode`) but **outlives a logout** (the owner, 24.09.2026): a crew member who signs out on a shared phone keeps his seat — on the screen of the session he ended last — and his name on the picker until he leaves the shift or it is closed, because a logout is not a leave (`seatsOn`). "Which shift is open?" is therefore no longer a question about the venue — `actorShift()` reads it off the `Actor`, and everything that writes money or stock asks that rather than `currentShift()`. A session lives until its own shift is closed, and *Zaključi smjenu* signs out **that crew only**. Tile colour, `shift_seq` and the *Smjene* slot name all come from the template's `sort`, and the shelf is shared while *Potrošeno u smjeni* is per shift. **The drawer float is not counted at all**: `openingFloat` is 0 unless an admin overrides it, because the crew empties the wallet at the handover and what stays behind is a change float nobody enters.
- **Sessions, not `user_id` in a body.** A phone enrols once with a six-character code and gets a device cookie; a person then PIN-logs in against that device. Every handler reads `event.context.actor` / `event.context.venueId`, which `tenant.ts` resolves from the cookies; no body ever names the actor and no handler reads a venue from a body or picks one itself. PINs are scrypt hashes with a pepper (`PIN_PEPPER`), every attempt at all three doors leaves an `auth_attempts` row, and lockout steps at 5 / 10 / 15 failures.
- **The PIN identifies the person.** The first screen is a pad and nothing else — no names, no role buttons. `POST /api/auth/pin` takes `{ pin, mode? }` and names nobody: the server compares the digits against every active account of the device's venue and 401s when none matches. So a PIN must be **unique among the active users of a venue** — `requirePinFree` refuses a duplicate at every door that sets one (409 `PIN_TAKEN`), and it is a check and not a unique index because a scrypt hash is salted per row and two hashes of one PIN never collide. Deactivated people hold no PIN against anybody, and a returning person's stale PIN is dropped rather than trusted. At that door there is no account to count failures against, so lockout keys on `(device, ip)` instead of `(device, user)`; the approver door still knows its user and still keys on `(device, user)`. A device lock therefore needs a device-shaped key: `unlockDevice` writes one (`clearDeviceCounter`), because clearing `locked_at` without the counter is an unlock that lasts one tap.
- The server never trusts a price or amount from a phone; prices are snapshotted at lock; stock is deducted inside the lock transaction; every phone-born row is idempotent (`UNIQUE(venue_id, client_id)`) and a replay returns 200 with the stored result, never 409.
- **One poll.** Every mutating transaction calls `bump(tx, …)`, which appends to `changes`; `GET /api/changes?since=<seq>` is the single timer on every screen (15 s), ETagged with the role *and* user in the tag. `tests/unit/changes-coverage.test.ts` walks `server/api/**` and fails on a mutation that does not bump or is not an exempted non-event.
- Migrations only (`drizzle-kit generate` + `migrate`); never `drizzle-kit push` against real data. Korak 2 is one migration, `0001_korak2.sql`; `0002_no_telegram.sql` drops `users.telegram_chat_id` with the notification path.
- Secrets live in `/opt/sank/.env` on the VPS only; nothing secret ships to the client. The one the app cannot run without is `PIN_PEPPER` — there is no session secret, because sessions are random tokens stored as sha256 and there is nothing to sign. `SANK_DEV_ENROL=1` is a dev-only door and is never set on the server. `.env.example` is the local list; `deploy/deploy.env.example` is the server's.
- Scheduled work is two Nitro tasks in one process (`server/tasks/{nightly,backup}.ts`), scheduled in UTC because `deploy/sank.service` sets `TZ=UTC`; anything that needs the café's wall clock goes through `shared/dates.ts`. They are no-ops under vitest.
- **Bosnian only, no i18n layer.** Every UI string and log title is written in Bosnian (ijekavian) directly in the code; no English on any screen, no translation library. English stays in identifiers, comments and docs. `PLAN.md` §12 is the glossary — reuse its labels verbatim.
- **Nothing notifies outward.** There is no Telegram, no e-mail, no push and no bot: the *Dnevnik* and the in-app *obavijesti* list on *Puls* are the only two channels the owner has, and there is no chat either — *Razgovor* was removed on the owner's call (15.09.2026). `alert_events` + `queueAlert` survive as the record behind that list — queued, deduped, never sent; `sent_at` is an unwritten leftover (BACKEND §9). Do not add a sender.
- One activity record: `log_entries` (written by `log()` inside the same transaction as the event, so an entry exists only if the event committed) feeds the owner-only *Dnevnik* page and that attention list; there is no separate `audit_log`. `shared/logTemplates.ts` is frozen for Korak 2: `kind` is typed, every kind has a Bosnian title template, and the ✔ column (`LOG[kind].alert`) names which of `ALERT_RULE_KEYS` it raises. Locks and payments are deliberately **not** entries — they are the ledger. Uploads and the roster tables follow the same venue_id / client_id conventions; the only upload kind is `delivery`. The `chat_*` tables and their triggers are left in the database with no migration, and nothing reads or writes them — do not bring a chat, a message or a system line back.
- The end of shift is one flow: the šanker's *Zaključi smjenu* (`POST /api/shifts/:id/zakljucenje`, šanker-mode sessions only), which also signs that shift's crew out and leaves the other shift working. It closes the whole shift with a subtraction — Sav prihod − Dnevnica (once per shift) − Otpis − Rashod − Policija − Osoblje − Merkator − Dodatna plaćanja = Za predati — and nobody types counted cash. **The server owns the first six** (the owner's call, 16.09.2026): *Otpis*, *Rashod*, *Policija* and *Osoblje* — every reason a tab may close authorised, `AUTHORISED_UNPAID_REASONS` — are marked on the floor plan's own sheet, counted by `shiftCategories` and subtracted by themselves the way *Dnevnica* is, so the phone sends only *Merkator* and *Dodatna plaćanja* (*Plaćanje robe / okusa / žara / kafe* were retired on 17.09.2026 — staff pay for none of them; the body accepts them only as 0 and the columns stay for older closings) — free lines, a label and an amount each ("config 15 KM"), stored as JSON on the closing and summed **by the server**. There is no *Napomena* on that screen any more. **Each category lives where it is drunk**: a table's sheet offers *Premjesti · Policija · Rashod*, and *Bez stola* — the guests at the bar, which is a tab, a card and the same sheet as any table, opened at `/konobar?sto=bez-stola` — offers *Premjesti · Rashod · Otpis · Osoblje*. **The bar on the floor plan is tapped too** (the owner, 22.09.2026): it opens *Šank* — *Stanje šanka* (the shelf, read-only, over the plan), *Nargila za ponijeti* and *Piće za ponijeti* (a tab on no table, the guest pays), and *Osoblje* and *Otpis*, which are the same menu carrying `?namjena=osoblje|otpis` and mark the tab the moment the round is locked, so the goods leave the shelf like any sale and the money leaves the shift instead of the waiter. A guest's table cannot be marked *Otpis* or *Osoblje* at all. `otpis_fen` is that plus `product_waste`. The konobar has no end-of-shift; the old blind settlement route (`/settle`) survives unused by the UI. Do not add a second end-of-shift; extend this one.
- **There is no *Dodatni žar*** (the owner, 25.09.2026). A table on the floor plan answers a tap and nothing else — no long press — and every lock goes through *Potvrdi*; the coal on a bowl is the bowl's own `coal_pcs` and nothing tops it up. `0025_no_zar.sql` switched the product off and cleared its key, so `system_key` has one value, `'ostalo'`, and `order_lines.parent_line_id` survives only for the rows locked before. Do not bring the top-up back.
- ***Moja smjena* is two things.** The articles the shift sold — grouped by name, with the quantity and what they took — and *Ukupan pazar* under them (the owner's call, 16.09.2026). The counts card, the thirty-night history with its *Napomena*, *Moji sati*, *Moji podaci* and the `/stavke` drill-down are gone, and so is the **blindness strip**: the total is readable while the night runs, because nobody declares any more. `summary` on `GET /api/me/shift` is still null until a settlement row exists, and `sold` carries no per-person split — the rows are the shift's. Do not put a second number on that screen.
- Receiving goods (*Prijem robe*, delivery photos) is admin-only; a šanker reads *Stanje šanka* and never adds to it. Workers never reach `/admin`.

## Sensitivity notes

- **Not a fiscal device.** No receipts, no printing, no PDV lines, no receipt numbering; the word *račun* appears only in "Na račun kuće". Keep the disclaimer in README and the watermark on *Pokaži narudžbu*.
- **Per-person views are private.** *Dnevnik* and drill-downs are owner-only; no per-person money ever appears on a staff screen other than that person's own. The honesty note about the database file is published in *Pravila* — keep it truthful.
- **Accountability, not surveillance.** Staff see their own numbers first; flags say "označeno za razgovor", never accuse; no leaderboards; thresholds are published in *Pravila* inside the app. Read PLAN.md §8 before touching any per-person metric.
