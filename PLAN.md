# Šank — Project Plan

Šank is an internal order, shift, bar-stock, team and owner-dashboard system for one shisha lounge in Bosnia and Herzegovina. Waiters lock rounds on their own phones; every KM and every gram enters once, as an immutable row attributed to a person and a device; the owner sees cash counted vs expected and stock counted vs expected per shift, and every per-person number is shown to that person first. It is not a fiscal cash register. This plan (2026-09-08) fixes the stack, data model, flows, accountability rules, UX, roadmap and open decisions after four reviews (thief, waiter, junior-dev, completeness); their critical and major findings are applied below or listed under §17. The owner's four café rules (daily deliveries from a photo, several owner accounts, tobacco in grams, category spend vs revenue) close §1 and are applied in F8, §9, §11 and §13. Vedran's second set of requirements (same day: group chat with images, an admin-only log channel, the waiter's shift lock-in with an automatic summary, an editable shift schedule, Bosnian only) is applied in F12–F14, §6, §10, §11 and §13 after three specialist designs (Razgovor, Dnevnik, Raspored) and three critiques (abuse, junior-dev, UX); every critical and major critique point is applied or rejected by name in §17.

Working name: Šank (šank = the bar counter). Folder: ~/Projects/sank. Rename is a one-line change.

## 1. Vision & the problem

Today nothing is recorded per order: waiters carry cash, the owner sees only the evening's *pazar*, and the *stanje šanka* is a notebook nobody trusts. The owner names two causes for the failing business — leakage by staff and a bad working environment — and they are one problem. Without a shared, trustworthy record of what was sold, by whom, for how much, and what left the shelf as a result, every shortage is an accusation from gut feeling and every honest waiter is a suspect.

Šank creates that record at the moment it happens and derives every number the owner sees from it. The product bet: accountability works only when it is symmetric and arrives early. So the accountability loop — lock a round, blind per-waiter cash handover, blind spot count of ~20 high-value items (weighed, not guessed), the owner's 5-minute *Pregled dana* — reaches real staff after Phase 2, before any analytics exist. What is not deferred is the shape of the data: append-only ledgers in feninga and base units, `venue_id` on every table, server-snapshotted prices and server-assigned numbers, stock movements written inside the lock transaction, client UUIDs on everything born on a phone. That shape is what lets everything deferred (split bills, modifier groups, SSE, history charts, a second venue) be added instead of rebuilt.

The app only sees what a waiter taps. The plan therefore pairs the ledger with physical checks it cannot fake: a weighed spot count including coffee and tobacco, coal per bowl, a bartender prep list ("no ticket, no drink") on nights with a *šanker*, and owner-run random counts. Rules the app cannot enforce are written into *Pravila* inside the app so nobody is surprised.

Design values, in priority order: FAST for waiters (two coffees in 5 taps, cash payment in 3, a coal top-up in 2); CLEAR for the owner (cash and stock, counted vs expected, per shift); HONEST for staff (thresholds published, "označeno za razgovor" never a verdict, no leaderboard, per-person custody so nobody pays another's *manjak*); SIMPLE for a junior developer (one process, one SQLite file, synchronous transactions, polling); a data model that survives a second venue.

**The owner's café rules (Vedran, 2026-09-08)** — four operating facts that shape the build; each is applied in the section named:
1. **Roba stiže svaki dan.** Merchandise can be ordered and delivered every day, so *Prijem robe* is a daily owner task, not a monthly one. Deliveries are entered by owners/admins only (default `bartender_can_receive_goods:false`), and the owner photographs the supplier's delivery note (*otpremnica/račun*) so the app pre-fills the lines — *Prijem sa slike*, F8 and §9. Nothing reaches the stock ledger without an owner tapping *Proknjiži*.
2. **Više vlasnika / admina.** Several people run the place; each has their own owner account, and every admin action (delivery, price change, void decision, count confirmation, settings edit, pickup) carries the person's name and appears in *Dnevnik* — §2, §11 page 6, F13.
3. **Nargila se prati u gramima.** The norm is 20 g per bowl, so 1 kg of a flavour = 50 bowls. Each month: opening g + received g − closing g (weighed) = used g; used ÷ 20 = expected bowls, compared with bowls actually locked. The closing weight carries over as next month's opening; flavour can be received on any day — §9 *Nargila*.
4. **Nabavka vs prodaja po kategoriji.** For every category the owner wants money spent on ordering next to money earned, to see where to improve. Products and stock items map to one shared list of categories (Vedran sends the list, §15 Q15) — §9 *Kategorije*, §11.

**Vedran's second set (2026-09-08)** — six requirements for the team side of the app, all mandatory; none may add a tap to the order flow:
- **R1 Razgovor.** Group chat inside the app with three fixed channels: *Admini* (owners only, invisible to staff), *Konobari* (staff only, invisible to owners in the app), *Svi* (everyone) — for "šta treba naručiti", confirmations, photos. F12, S15–S16, §8 fairness contract.
- **R2 Dnevnik.** Every activity is logged where the other admins can see it; admins only. The *Aktivnost* page and `audit_log` become one thing, `log_entries` and the *Dnevnik* page — F13, §11 page 6.
- **R3 Završi smjenu.** The waiter ends ("locks in") his shift; the app writes his promet automatically ("612,50 KM"), posts it to *Dnevnik*, and every category on that entry ("Nargila 34") opens the list of those lines with the time each one was locked. The lock-in *is* the existing per-waiter settlement (F10) — extended, not duplicated — F13.
- **R4 Slike i kamera.** The chat takes photos from the camera or the gallery (a nice coffee, a mess left by the previous shift) — F12 (c), `uploads` in §6, shared with the delivery-note scans.
- **R5 Raspored smjena.** A shift schedule that is fast to edit and lets staff swap shifts, because swapping is common here — F14, S17, §11.
- **R6 Samo bosanski.** No English in the UI and no i18n layer: strings are written in Bosnian in the code; English only in identifiers, comments and developer docs — §4, §5, §10.

Above all: the waiter flow must be faster than paper, or nothing else here gets data (§10).

## 2. Who uses it

`users.role ∈ owner | manager | bartender | waiter`; `manager` is reserved in the enum with no UI in v1. Staff log in with name + PIN on an enrolled device (4 digits for waiters, 6 for owner and bartender — `users.pin_len`); the owner additionally has email + password for `/admin`. Deactivation (`users.active = 0`) replaces deletion everywhere. There can be several `owner` users (co-owners and admins, owner's rule 2); "the owner" anywhere in this plan means any of them. Every admin write carries `actor_id`, the row shows the person's name, and *Dnevnik* (§11 page 6, F13) lists who did what; each owner links their own Telegram (`users.telegram_chat_id`) and receives every alert; the "own device" approval rule (§5) applies to each owner separately. A restricted admin (an accountant with read-only access, say) is the `manager` role, UI in Phase 5; in chat a `manager` sees *Svi* only and never *Dnevnik* (§15 Q18).

**Nights with no šanker.** Venue settings decide who can do what: `approver_roles` (default `['owner','bartender']`); the shift opens itself on the first lock (F1), so `waiter_can_open_shift` is gone; a lone closer settles to the safe (F10). Without an approver on the floor, voids and comps stay `pending` in the waiter's expected cash until the owner decides — from his own phone, in seconds if he is present, next morning if not.

What nobody can do, including the owner: edit or delete a locked line, order, payment, settlement, log entry or stock movement; backdate anything; reopen a closed shift or paid tab; change a closed shift except by annotating and reviewing it; read the *Konobari* channel from the app. Database triggers enforce this (§6), not only code. UI rule: nothing forbidden is greyed out silently; the sheet says "Ovo potvrđuje šanker/vlasnik" and offers the path — never "nemate pravo".

## 3. Not a fiscal device

Šank is an internal order, stock and accountability record. It issues no receipts, prints nothing and produces no PDF in v1. The guest-facing *Pokaži narudžbu* view is a phone screen watermarked `PREGLED NARUDŽBE · NIJE FISKALNI RAČUN · NIJE NARUDŽBENICA`. There are no PDV lines, no receipt numbering, no QR codes; the word *račun* appears in the UI only inside *Na račun kuće*. `tabs.fiscal_status` (`none`) and `fiscal_ref` are the only fiscal columns and are stubs for a future integration.

Reviewer-reported, not re-verified here: FBiH adopted a new *Zakon o fiskalizaciji transakcija* in January 2026 (in force February 2026, application within 18 months) that regulates a pre-receipt *narudžbenica* in hospitality and points toward e-fiscalization. Whether and when the lounge fiscalizes sales is the owner's legal decision, outside this app; the ledger records what was sold and does not make it fiscal. The entity (FBiH / RS / Brčko) decides the eventual integration path (§15 Q13). README carries the same disclaimer.

| API | Nitro server routes; `server/services/*` as pure functions taking `tx` and `venueId` | testable against `:memory:` with real migrations |

| Auth | cookie sessions, scrypt (`node:crypto`), device tokens | no third-party auth to pay for |

| Images | `<input type="file" capture>` + canvas downscale on the phone; multipart to one Nitro route; JPEG files under `/var/lib/sank/uploads`, served only through an access-checked route; no `sharp`, no native module | chat photos (R4) and delivery-note scans share one `uploads` table and one route; nothing under `public/` |
| Receipt scanning | Claude API (`claude-opus-5`, vision + structured output to a Zod schema) from one Nitro route via `@anthropic-ai/sdk`; key in `.env` on the VPS | daily deliveries entered from a photo of the otpremnica, owner confirms every line (F8); ~0,03 USD per scan, under 1 USD a month |
| Tests | vitest, unit + service tests against real migrations | Phase 0 tests are the guardrail for the ledger |
| Hosting | Ubuntu VPS, systemd (`Restart=always`), rsync deploy, HTTPS; nginx `client_max_body_size 4m` for uploads | ~5 EUR/month; secure origin needed for the service worker |
| Backups | `db.backup()` hourly + daily, `rclone` to Cloudflare R2 (database + delivery photos; chat photos stay on the VPS) | free 10 GB; restore rehearsed before pilot |

Why not the alternatives: Supabase moves the one hard piece (atomic lock → price snapshot → stock deduction → `shift_seq`) into plpgsql or edge functions, and the free tier pauses after idle days; PocketBase is pre-1.0 with a Go hooks layer; Firebase is hostile to ledgers; Workers + D1 has no better-sqlite3. SQL in queries stays portable so a Postgres swap remains open; triggers live in one SQL file. Why no chat service or WebSocket server for R1: eight phones and one owner produce a few messages an hour; a table, a sequence number and the poll that already runs every 15 s carry it, and the access matrix stays in one function next to the rest of the tenant code.

## 5. Architecture

### Topology and ownership
One VPS, one Node process, one file `/var/lib/sank/sank.db` (`chmod 600`), separate Linux user and `sank.service` (`Restart=always`, `RestartSec=5`, `Environment=TZ=UTC` — business dates never come from the process zone). Reverse proxy with HTTPS and `client_max_body_size 4m` (chat and scan uploads). No Redis, no queue, no PM2 cluster; README says so. Deploy = `rsync` + `drizzle-kit migrate` + `applyTriggers` + `systemctl restart` in `deploy.sh`; never `drizzle-kit push` against real data. Domain and VPS are either in the owner's name with Vedran as admin or covered by a written note that the DB file and backups are the owner's and handed over on request (§15 Q11); a second person holds SSH and R2 credentials in a shared password-manager vault. `deploy/OWNER_RUNBOOK.md`, one page in Bosnian: "aplikacija ne radi" → paper blok, back-fill as `source='manual'` next day; how to restart the service; how to download last night's backup; whom to call.

### Language
The UI is Bosnian (ijekavian) and nothing else (R6). There is no i18n layer: every label, toast, error copy, system message and log title is written in Bosnian where it is used — Vue templates, `shared/logTemplates.ts` (Dnevnik titles), `shared/chatSystem.ts` (chat system lines) and `shared/errors.ts` (the map from API error codes such as `PENDING_OUTBOX` to the sentence the phone shows). Identifiers, column names, error codes, comments, commit messages, PLAN.md and README stay English. An eslint rule forbids importing any i18n package; a test greps `app/` templates for a short list of English words that leak from habit, as whole words in template text (`Save`, `Cancel`, `Delete`, `Loading`, `Error`, `Yes`, `No`, `Submit`, `Search`, `Settings`, `Close`, `Back`, `Send` — `OK` is not on the list because it is the flag-outcome word) and fails on a hit. Telegram messages are Bosnian too: they are rendered from the same `title_bs` templates (F13 a). Vocabulary is fixed in §12; new words go there before they go in code.

### Backups and integrity
Nitro tasks: `db.backup()` hourly 12:00–04:00 (keep 48) and daily 05:00 (keep 60); daily `rclone copy` to R2 of the database files plus the delivery photos only — the list of files is generated from `uploads WHERE kind='delivery' AND deleted_at IS NULL` (`rclone --files-from`), so nothing reaches R2 that no `delivery_scans` row references; `uploads/chat/` is never copied offsite (F12 c). Rule: a backup is not done until a restore has been rehearsed; the monthly drill (pull offsite file → `PRAGMA integrity_check` → run locally → compare last `shift_seq` and yesterday's pazar) is dated in `deploy/RESTORE.md`. Nightly 05:30 task recomputes every closed shift's summary from ledger rows (the numeric tree of `shift_summaries`, including `by_category_json` and `by_user_json`, compared with a deterministic key order) and compares with the latest `shift_summaries` version; drift → `log_entries(kind='drift')` + Telegram `drift`. Because post-close decisions write a new summary version (§6), drift means a code bug, never a legitimate event. Hourly `server/tasks/uploads_gc.ts` unlinks the file of every upload older than 60 minutes that no non-deleted `chat_messages` row references and no `delivery_scans` row with status `parsed|applied` references (an `uploaded` or `discarded` scan does not keep a file alive), and sets `uploads.deleted_at` — it never DELETEs a row, `uploads` being one-transition (both kinds); nightly 05:40 `retention.ts` (Phase 3b) unlinks chat images older than 90 days; text redaction (12 months) and device rows (24 months) join the Phase 5 retention task — nothing reaches those ages before then.

### Triggers outside Drizzle
drizzle-kit does not manage triggers and rebuilds SQLite tables (`__new_x` copy + rename) for many column changes, silently dropping triggers (assumption consistent with every release to date). So `server/db/triggers.sql` holds every trigger as `DROP TRIGGER IF EXISTS … ; CREATE TRIGGER …` and `applyTriggers(db)` runs it at process start after `migrate()` (idempotent, ~50 ms). Ledger table names and trigger names live in `shared/constants.ts`; a test enumerates both and fails when a ledger table has no trigger or a trigger is missing from `sqlite_master`.

### Auth
Owner: email + scrypt password, cookie `sank_s`, 30-day sliding. Staff: owner generates a 6-char enrol code (10 min TTL, valid for 2 uses so an iOS install mistake costs nothing); the phone gets `sank_d` (365 days, `devices.mode personal|shared`, personal bound to one user); name + PIN → 14 h session. A personal device also offers *Drugi konobar* → name → PIN → `sessions.borrowed=1`, 2 h TTL, header "Amar · Emirov telefon"; `device_id` stays the phone's, `user_id` the borrower's; listed as "posuđen telefon" (informational). Shared devices re-lock after `shared_device_idle_s` (300); the lock screen shows the last 3 users as avatars, PIN auto-submits on the last digit. Offline unlock on shared devices (Phase 2): a WebCrypto PBKDF2 hash of the PIN per (user, device) cached for users who logged in there in the last 14 h; the local session is re-validated by the next online request. Lockout keyed by `device_id + user_id`: 5 fails → 60 s, 10 → 15 min + Telegram, 15 → device locked. Revoked device → `401 DEVICE_REVOKED` → client wipes IndexedDB. `server/middleware/tenant.ts` resolves `venueId` from session/device token into `event.context`; no handler reads `venue_id` from a body. Rate limits: `/api/auth/*` 10/min per IP, `/api/orders` 60/min per device, `/api/chat/messages` 10/min per device, `/api/uploads` 10/min per device. *Moji podaci* (S11) lists the user's own sessions (device label, first and last seen), so a login from a device that is not his is visible to him — the "Identitet" metric (§8) adds "novi korisnik prijavljen na uređaju vlasnika".

**Approvals.** The owner decides only on his own device: `/admin` *Zahtijeva pažnju* (Telegram deep link) or his own phone in `/konobar`. His PIN is never accepted on a device bound to someone else or on a shared staff device — a PIN typed on a waiter's phone is captured once and approves everything afterwards. The bartender may approve on the waiter's phone with his 6-digit PIN; every decision records `decided_on_device_id`, and a decision on a device other than the approver's own gets `foreign_device=1`, shows in *Zahtijeva pažnju* ("Odobreno na Amarovom telefonu 22:41 · storno 12,00 KM") and sends Telegram for any `was_paid=1` void. Server rejects `approved_by = requested_by` for everyone except the owner, whose self-approvals are listed as "Odobrio sam sebi". Offline, an approver PIN cannot be verified, so the request is queued `pending` with copy "Nema veze — ide na čekanje, Emir potvrđuje sa svog telefona"; the šanker's `/konobar` has a *Na čekanju (2)* badge with one-tap *Odobri* for requests whose line was locked ≤ 900 s ago (`bartender_approve_window_s`, checked at decision time); older requests show "Ide vlasniku" and only the owner can decide them.

### Offline
**Drafts** never leave the phone until lock: Pinia `cart` persisted with `idb-keyval`, key `draft:{user_id}:{table_id}`. The phone mints `tab_client_id` (uuid) the first time it opens S2 for a table with no known open tab and stores it in the draft; every `order_lines.id` is a client uuid too. So payments, unpaid marks and voids can be queued for a tab and lines the server has not seen yet.

**Outbox — money and stock only.** Pinia `outbox` persisted with `idb-keyval`; entries `{client_id, kind: order|pay|unpaid|adjust|waste, tab_client_id?, payload, client_created_at, attempts, last_error}`. Flush sequentially oldest-first on `online`, `visibilitychange`, after every enqueue and every 10 s while visible; `AbortSignal.timeout(8000)` per POST (`4000` for polling), a timeout counts as a network error; back-off 10 s → 60 s, reset on `online`/`visibilitychange`/any success; flush runs before polling. On 2xx or `409 ALREADY_APPLIED` remove. On 4xx the entry becomes `failed` and blocks every later entry of the same `tab_client_id` until the waiter acknowledges ("Popravi ili odbaci"); other tabs continue. `navigator.storage.persist()` requested at first login; S11 shows "Trajno spremanje: uključeno/isključeno". Server guarantees: `UNIQUE(venue_id, client_id)` on `tabs`, `orders`, `payments`, `line_adjustments`, `waste_events`; a test replays every mutation twice and expects one row. The outbox is still volatile (dead phone, cleared site data) — the reconciliation (§8), not the phone, is the accountability mechanism, and heartbeat gaps are recorded. **Chat never enters this store**: unsent messages and photos live in the chat store's own pending list (`chat:pending` in `idb-keyval`, F12 d), are never counted in `outbox_len`, the heartbeat's `pending`, the sync chip, the logout gate or the settlement and count gates — a stuck "nema leda" must not stop a cash handover.

**Heartbeat.** `POST /api/devices/heartbeat` every 60 s while visible and after every successful flush: `{pending, oldest_pending_at, client_now, app_version, standalone}` — `pending` counts money and stock entries only. Server stores `pending_count`, `last_seen_at`, `clock_skew_s = server_now − client_now`. Every outbox payload's `client_created_at` is adjusted by the skew (`client_created_at_adj`) before any comparison; `|skew| > 300` is an attention line and a `clock_skew` log entry "Amarov telefon: sat kasni 6 min". A gap > 10 min during an open shift writes `order_events(type='device_offline', {from,to})` and a `device_offline` log entry, shown on the waiter's row and summed per 30 days. A device is *fresh* when `last_seen_at` is within `heartbeat_fresh_s` (600), *stale* otherwise; settlement (F10) and counts (F9) inspect the heartbeat of every device that locked a round for the user (or any shift member) in this shift, not only the device sending the request.

**No shared mutable document.** Clients only append; the server owns tabs. Two waiters on table 5 each send their own `tab_client_id`; the server resolves both to the single open tab (by `tab_client_id`, else by open tab on the table) and returns the canonical id, which the phone adopts. Late-sync rules are in F3/F5.

**Visible truth.** Sync chip in every `/konobar` header: green "Sinkronizovano", amber "Čeka slanje (2)", red "Nema veze — narudžbe se čuvaju". Red never blocks adding, locking or paying; a queued payment keeps the table yellow "Naplata čeka slanje". Unsent round older than 5 min → banner; logout blocked with "Imaš 2 neposlane narudžbe" (chat pending never counts). Lock toast reads outbox state: "Poslano · Sto 7 · Tura 1" vs "Sačuvano · čeka slanje · Sto 7".

**PWA.** Precache the shell, `NetworkFirst` (3 s) for `/api/bootstrap`, `NetworkOnly` otherwise; "Nova verzija — osvježi" only between orders. iOS: the home-screen app has its own cookie jar and IndexedDB (assumption from documented WebKit behaviour; verify on one iPhone in Phase 1), so on iOS the browser page shows only the 3-picture *Dodaj na početni ekran* guide and enrolment runs inside the installed app; Android works in either order. `navigator.vibrate` only where `'vibrate' in navigator`; visual flash otherwise. No Background Sync on iOS: the chip says "Otvori aplikaciju da pošalješ". A memory-pressure reload after the iOS camera loses nothing: drafts, the outbox, the chat pending list and the chat composer draft (`chat_draft:{channel}`) are all in IndexedDB.

### Realtime
v1 is polling. Owner *Uživo* fetches `/api/owner/live` every 15 s with `If-None-Match`/ETag; `/konobar` fetches `/api/tables/state` on open, on `visibilitychange` and every 15 s while visible; "Ažurirano 22:41" always shown. Both responses carry `chat_max_seq` (one `MAX(seq)` lookup) and, for owners, `log_max_at`; the client calls `GET /api/chat/since` or `/api/owner/log?after=` only when the value moved past its cursor — no second timer anywhere (F12 e). `bus.emit(venueId, event)` call sites sit in `server/services/*` from day one (orders, payments, chat, log, roster) so a Phase 5 SSE endpoint touches no business logic. No WebSockets.

### Time
`venues.settings_json.timezone = 'Europe/Sarajevo'`; `shared/dates.ts` exports `businessDate(utcIso, tz, startHour)` using `Intl.DateTimeFormat` parts (no library), tested for 05:59 vs 06:00 local and both DST nights (2026-10-25 02:30 ambiguous, 2027-03-28 02:30 non-existent), plus `localTime(utcIso, tz)` for "21:47" and `weekStart(date)` (Monday) for the roster. No business code calls `getHours()` without a zone (eslint `no-restricted-syntax`). `shifts.business_date` is computed once from `opened_at` (or the first lock's `client_created_at_adj` on auto-open) and never re-derived. Money: `shared/money.ts` custom formatter (`125050 → "1.250,50 KM"`), unit-tested, not `Intl` currency (ICU output may vary across Node builds — assumption). Dates `08.09.2026.`, times 24 h, decimal inputs accept `,` and `.`.

## 6. Data model

Conventions: `id` is a text UUID (client-generated for anything born in the outbox or the chat pending list — tabs, orders, order_lines, payments, adjustments, waste, chat_messages — server-generated otherwise); every business table has `venue_id TEXT NOT NULL` and every unique index starts with `venue_id`, with three documented global exceptions: `UNIQUE(enrol_codes.code)`, `UNIQUE(users.email) WHERE email IS NOT NULL`, `UNIQUE(venues.slug)` — a phone enrolling has no venue yet. A vitest introspects `sqlite_master` and fails if a table lacks `venue_id` or a unique index outside the allowed list exists. Money `INTEGER` feninga (`*_fen`); quantities `REAL` in base units; timestamps UTC ISO set by the server; `client_created_at` and `client_created_at_adj` kept where a phone originated the row. JSON columns that feed comparisons hold ids and integers only — never names, which are joined at read time (users and categories are never deleted): `shift_summaries.*_json` is purely numeric (the nightly recompute compares it); `waiter_settlements.summary_json` follows the same rule for every amount and count and additionally carries the two window timestamps (`joined_at`, `settled_at`, ISO) and the `within_tolerance` boolean, which no recompute compares — its test is byte-identity (F13 e).

**Append-only (ledger) tables** — `BEFORE UPDATE`/`BEFORE DELETE` → `RAISE(ABORT,'append-only')`: `orders`, `order_lines`, `order_line_flavours`, `payments`, `stock_movements`, `stock_count_lines`, `order_events`, `log_entries` (with the one redaction transition below), `staff_notes`, `price_history`, `shift_summaries`, `delivery_lines` (after post). **One-transition tables** — trigger allows exactly one status move and only the named columns (every other column `OLD.x IS NEW.x`): `line_adjustments` (`pending → applied|rejected`; `status, approved_by, decided_on_device_id, foreign_device, decided_at, decided_in_shift_id, restock`), `cash_movements` (`pending → approved|rejected`), `waste_events` (`approved_by` null → set), `waiter_settlements` (`accepted_by, accepted_at` null → set, once, nothing else — the owner's acceptance of a self-sealed handover; every other correction is a new row with `supersedes_id`), `stock_counts` (`draft → submitted → confirmed`), `shifts` (once `closed`: only `status closed→reviewed`, `reviewed_by`, `reviewed_at`, `closing_note`, `card_total_fen`), `tabs` (once `paid|voided`: only `pending_review`, `late_sync`, `unpaid_*`), `chat_messages` (`deleted_at, deleted_by` null → set once; separately `redacted_at, body` null → set once to the fixed form "Uklonjeno · retencija", used only by the Phase 5 retention task), `log_entries` (`redacted_at, title_bs, body_json` once, same purpose), `uploads` (`deleted_at` null → set), `swap_requests` (`pending → accepted|declined|cancelled`; `status, to_user_id, decided_by, decided_at`). A request/decision table pair would be purer, but its join lands in every total a junior writes. **Plainly mutable, by design and documented as such**: `chat_channels` (pinned note), `chat_reads` (read positions), `shift_templates`, `roster_weeks`, `roster_assignments` — a schedule and a read cursor are plans and bookmarks, not ledgers; every roster write goes through `services/roster.ts`, which writes a `log_entries` row with before/after in the same transaction (F14).

**Cross-cutting.** `staff_notes(id, venue_id, ref_type, ref_id, user_id, text, created_at)`. `alert_events(id, venue_id, rule_key, ref_type, ref_id, payload_json, sent_at, acknowledged_at?, acknowledged_by?, outcome ok|opomena|greska_aplikacije?, outcome_note?)`, `UNIQUE(venue_id, rule_key, ref_type, ref_id)`. There is no `audit_log`: `log_entries` is the one who-did-what ledger (R2), and the *Dnevnik* page is its one reader.

**Invariants** (each has a Phase 0 test): tab total (reporting) = Σ `charged_fen` − Σ applied adjustment `amount_fen`; tab remaining (what the guest still pays, F5) = tab total − Σ `pending` void `amount_fen` − Σ `payments.amount_fen`; stock on hand = `SUM(qty_delta)` for every page (kept true by `late_sync` offsets); `expectedCash(tx, shiftId, userId?)` is one function used by every view, with `venueExpected = drawerExpected + Σ waiterExpected` (terms in F10); `orders.shift_id` is never null; a locked row is never mutated; every phone-born row has a client uuid; every price on a line was copied by the server; Σ `by_user.promet_fen` = Σ `by_category.fen` = `shift_summaries.promet_fen`; a `log_entries` row exists only inside a committed event transaction; a `chat_messages` row of a channel the requester may not see is never returned by any route, and no `*_fen` value is ever written into a *Svi* or *Konobari* message by the system.

## 7. Core flows

**F1 — Smjena se otvara sama (shift open).** 1) A shift exists when someone locks a round: inside `POST /api/orders`, if no shift is `open|closing`, insert `shifts(opened_by = locker, auto_opened = 1, business_date from client_created_at_adj)` and the locker's `shift_members` row (the first-action rule of §6 — a šanker who submits the opening count before any lock gets his row from that count), then proceed; `log(shift_opened)` in the same transaction, mirrored to Telegram "Smjena otvorena · Amar · 18:03 · automatski (prva tura)". Nobody ever sees "Smjena nije otvorena". 2) Opening float is derived (§6); if it cannot be derived, *Zahtijeva pažnju* shows "Unesi početni polog". 3) *Dopuni smjenu* by šanker/owner (on nights without a šanker, the first waiter in — §15 Q2): `float_out` per waiter (change from the drawer; `log(float_moved)`, quiet, also written for `float_in`), and the opening spot count — a blind `stock_counts(kind='spot', phase='open')` whose counter becomes `stock_custodian_id`; it writes no movements until the owner confirms. The opening count is required, not optional: a shift without one cannot close except by owner override (F10 step 4), because variance = close − open − sales + waste is attributable to one person only when the shift is bracketed. 4) Explicit `POST /api/shifts/open` exists for the same purpose before the first guest. 5) After a `closed` shift, the next lock auto-opens a new shift with the same `business_date` if before the cutoff — late guests are recorded, never hidden.

**F2 — Nova tura (take order).** 1) *Stolovi* → tap *Sto 7*. 2) Empty table → picker (S3) opens on the last-used tab, with a persistent "Sto 7 · Unutra" pill (tap to re-key the draft if wrong); occupied → S2 with *+ Dodaj*. 3) Tap *Kafa* twice (badge 2), tap *Coca-Cola*; feedback is the tile badge and strip pulse — no per-add toast. 4) Long-press = note chips from `categories.note_chips_json` (bez šećera · s mlijekom · dupla) or free text, or *Na račun kuće*; the *Nargila* tile opens S4. 5) The draft lives only on this phone; S1 shows "nacrt 3 · 8,50" on the tile, a header "Nacrti (2)", and a draft older than 15 min pulses "Sto 7: nacrt čeka — Zaključi?".

**F3 — Zaključi (lock).** 1) *Zaključi (3) · 8,50 KM* → sheet lists every line and the total → *Potvrdi*. 2) Enqueue; haptic where available; toast per outbox state. 3) `POST /api/orders`, one transaction: auto-open shift if needed (F1); `409 SETTLED` if this user already has a settlement in the open shift; resolve tab by `tab_client_id`, else open tab on the table, else create (`tabs.client_id` = phone's); insert `orders` with `shift_seq = MAX+1`; insert `order_lines` with client ids and `name_snapshot`, `unit_price_fen`, `charged_fen` copied from `products` now; resolve recipes and insert `sale` movements (`occurred_at = client_created_at_adj`, plus a `late_sync` offset per component when that `occurred_at` precedes a confirmed count — §9); `order_events(type='lock')`; if the sending device is bound to a user who already settled in this shift and the session belongs to somebody else, also `order_events(type='lock_after_settle_foreign')` (F13); `bus.emit`. Returns `total_fen`; if it differs from the draft total, the phone shows an amber card "Cijena promijenjena: Kafa 1,50 → 2,00 KM · Sto 7" and refetches `/api/bootstrap`. 4) Replay with the same `client_id` → `409 ALREADY_APPLIED`. 5) Late rules: `tab_client_id` matches a `paid` tab, or `client_created_at_adj < closed_at` of the table's last closed tab → new tab `status='unpaid', pending_review=1, late_sync=1, unpaid_reason='late_sync'` (never `open`, so the one-open-tab index and shift close are untouched); the phone gets a red card "Tura 21:50 za Sto 5 stigla nakon naplate (Emir 22:00). Jesi li naplatio?" → *Naplaćeno gotovina* (payment) / *Nije naplaćeno*; unanswered 10 min → šanker's queue. A lock with a freshly minted `tab_client_id` and `client_created_at_adj` after the paid tab's `closed_at` is the normal case: a new `open` tab on the table. No open shift and `client_created_at_adj` inside a closed shift's window → attach to that shift with `late_sync=1` as above, `order_events(type='late_after_close')`, `log(late_after_close)`, new summary version; the owner sees "2 ture stigle nakon zatvaranja · Amar · 38,00 KM — objašnjava višak". 6) A mistaken lock is fixed by the self-void (F6, `wrong_entry`); there is no second undo mechanism.

**F4 — Druga tura / Žar.** A second round is F2–F3 on the same tab. Coal: long-press a table with an active shisha on S1 (or tap *Žar* inline on the shisha line in S2) → sheet "Sto 7 · Nargila (jabuka)" → *Žar* locks immediately as its own `orders` row (system product "Dodatni žar", 0 KM, recipe 2 kom coal, `parent_line_id`) — 2 taps, 3 with two shishas. *Nova lula* (charged, own grams) goes through the normal lock. The one-confirm carve-out applies only to `charged_fen = 0` system products.

**F5 — Naplati (pay).** 1) S2 with no draft shows *+ Dodaj* | *Naplati 17,50*. 2) Sheet: cash chips *tačno · 20 · 50 · 100 · numpad*; *Kartica* chip only if `payment_methods` includes it; a line list with checkboxes as an amount helper for "svako svoje" (ticking sets `amount_fen`, header "Ostalo 12,50 KM"; the ticks are not stored — only order ids travel, no data split). 3) Tapping a chip commits: `POST /api/payments` with `covers_order_client_ids[]` = the orders on the tab the payer is settling (all of them by default; stored as-is in `payments.covers_json`); kusur shown big on the toast; tip only if `track_cash_tips`. Cash exact: Sto + Naplati + tačno = 3 taps. 4) Server: `amount_fen ≤ remaining` (remaining = tab total − Σ `pending` void amounts − Σ payments, §6 — the guest pays only what was served; a line under a pending void is not collected from him and stays in the requester's expected cash until decided, F6) else `422 OVERPAY`; tab `paid` inside the transaction when remaining hits 0; a paid tab stays paid — a void on it rejected later leaves the amount in the requester's expected (F10) and is listed "storno odbijen · nije naplaćeno 5,00 (Amar)"; already paid → `409 TAB_ALREADY_PAID {paid_by, at}` (shown, not silently dropped, `order_events(type='pay_duplicate_attempt')`, attention line); orders on the tab locked by another user and outside `covers` while remaining stays > 0 → payment recorded, tab stays `open` with `pending_review=1`, both waiters get a card "Sto 5: tura od Emira (22:03) nije pokrivena — 7,00 KM". `payments.shift_id` = the tab's shift when `client_created_at_adj < that shift's closed_at`, else the open shift. A lock after payment follows F3 step 5: fresh `tab_client_id` and a later timestamp → new `open` tab; the paid tab's `tab_client_id` or an earlier timestamp → `unpaid/late_sync`. 5) Guest walked out: *⋯ → Nije plaćeno* → reason → `tabs.status='unpaid', pending_review=1, unpaid_by = me`; allowed only by the current `assigned_to`; `log(unpaid_marked)` (quiet); its remaining balance stays in `unpaid_by`'s expected cash until the owner decides from *Zahtijeva pažnju* via `POST /api/tabs/:id/unpaid/decide {outcome: otpis|naplatiti}` — *otpis* sets `pending_review=0` and `unpaid_approved_by`, so the balance leaves the marker's expected cash (the venue writes it off); *naplatiti* changes nothing on the tab (the balance stays in the marker's expected until a payment closes the tab); both write `log(unpaid_decided)` resolving `unpaid_marked`. Reversal method for later voids: cash if the tab has any cash payment, else card.

**F6 — Storno (void).** 1) Long-press a locked line → "Zaključene stavke se ne mijenjaju" → *Zatraži storno*. 2) Reason chips `wrong_entry · guest_changed_mind · not_served · complaint · other` (note ≥ 5 chars for `other`); `walked_out` is not a void — it is *Nije plaćeno*. The sheet shows "Vraća robu na stanje: da/ne" from `restock` (`wrong_entry`, `guest_changed_mind`, `not_served` → 1; `complaint`, `other` → 0; approver may flip). 3) Server: own line, tab unpaid, `seconds_since_lock ≤ 300` → `applied, auto=1`; bartender PIN on this phone and `seconds_since_lock ≤ 900` → `applied` with `foreign_device`; otherwise `pending`, line struck amber "Storno čeka odobrenje — 5,00 KM ostaje u tvom pazaru dok se ne odobri"; `log(void_requested)` (quiet) on every non-auto request, `log(void_decided)` on every decision with `resolves_id` pointing at the request. A pending request can still be decided by the šanker from his own *Na čekanju* queue while `seconds_since_lock ≤ 900` at decision time; after that only the owner decides. 4) `applied` with `restock=1` writes one `sale_storno` per recipe component (`ref_type='line_adjustment'`, `qty_delta = +component × qty`, cost copied from the matching sale row, plus a `late_sync` offset when a confirmed count sits after its `occurred_at`); `was_paid=1` decided before the requester's settlement in that shift writes the reversing `payments` row (money physically returned by the waiter). 5) Decided after the requester's settlement (later that night or after close): no reversal row — money is not returned by the app; instead the requester's `waiterExpected` drops by `amount_fen` (the applied-after-settlement term in F10), so a waiter who refunded the guest from his pouch sees his derived diff clear, and one who handed the money over shows a višak listed as explained ("storno odobren nakon predaje · 5,00"), never flagged; the decision also writes a quiet `settlement_updated` entry under his *Završena smjena* entry (F13) so the Dnevnik card shows "nakon predaje: storno odobren −5,00". A rejection moves nothing. The owner may tick "Vraćeno gostu iz kase" → `cash_movements(type='refund')` in the current shift (reduces that shift's `drawerExpected`). If the shift is closed, the decision writes a new `shift_summaries` version. Every `was_paid=1` void and every `pending` request lands in *Zahtijeva pažnju*.

**F7 — Kuća časti (comp).** Draft line: long-press → *Na račun kuće* → `staff_drink | owner_guest | complaint | promo`; `staff_drink` is self-authorised for `staff_drink_allowed` products up to `staff_drink_max_fen` (3 KM) and `staff_drinks_per_shift` (2), sheet shows "Osoblje: 1/2 (do 3 KM)"; other reasons need an approver (`owner_guest` only the owner, from his own device — otherwise `pending` with `log(comp_requested)`, quiet); the line locks with list `unit_price_fen`, `charged_fen = 0`, `comp_reason`, `authorised_by`. Locked line: `line_adjustments(kind='comp', restock=0)` — the drink was served, so stock is never re-added; if paid, the money side follows F6. Decisions write `log(comp_decided)` resolving the `comp_requested` entry.

**F8 — Prijem robe (delivery, daily).** Deliveries arrive most days (owner's rule 1), so this is a one-to-three-minute owner task, admin-only by default (`bartender_can_receive_goods:false`). 1) *Roba → Prijem → Novi prijem* (also a shortcut on the phone *Puls* header): supplier defaults to the last one, date to today. 2) **Sa slike** (Phase 3): the owner photographs the otpremnica/račun with the same capture and downscale as chat photos (F12 c) at `maxEdge` 1600 px and JPEG quality 0.85 — small print on an A4 otpremnica stays legible for vision OCR; the phone posts it to `POST /api/uploads` with `kind='delivery'` (owner sessions only, or the šanker when `bartender_can_receive_goods` — `422 KIND_FORBIDDEN` otherwise), then `POST /api/stock/deliveries/scan {upload_id}` asks Claude (vision, structured output) for `{supplier, invoice_no, date, lines[{text, qty, pack, unit_price}]}` with the venue's stock items and `supplier_aliases` in the prompt; each line comes back matched to a `stock_item` with a confidence, or as *Nepoznata stavka*. The draft opens fully editable: matched lines green, unsure ones amber with the OCR text beside them, unknown ones need *Poveži* (pick the item — the alias is learned for next time), *Novi artikal* or *Preskoči*. Nothing is posted until the owner has looked at every line. 3) **Ručno** is the same form typed: item search, packs + loose with a per-line pack-size override, price per pack. 4) *Proknjiži* → one `delivery` movement per line with `unit_cost_fen` per base unit and `occurred_at = delivered_at`; moving average updated; `deliveries.posted_by` records which owner; `log(delivery_posted)` in the same transaction; the scan's status becomes `applied`. A scan that yields no posted delivery is `discarded` with a reason (`log(delivery_discarded)`); its upload file is unlinked by the hourly `uploads_gc` (a `discarded` scan does not keep a file alive, §5; the `uploads` row stays with `deleted_at` set). 5) Posted deliveries are reversed only by `correction` (owner, note). A delivery dated before the last confirmed count is listed as *Već prebrojano* and excluded from the next theoretical by `occurred_at`. 6) *Prijemi* lists the month with totals per category (§9 *Kategorije*) and a *Ručno uneseno* filter for deliveries without a photo.

**F9 — Brzi popis (spot count).** 1) The `is_spot` list (~20 items, §9), one screen, big number field, packs/loose toggle; `count_method='weigh'` items (open tobacco tins, coffee, sugar, open spirits) take grams on a kitchen scale with `tare_g` subtracted; theoretical hidden until submit. 2) Submit and confirm refuse with `409 PENDING_OUTBOX` while any device that locked a round in this shift has a fresh heartbeat (≤ `heartbeat_fresh_s`) with `pending_count > 0` (money and stock entries only — a pending chat message never counts); owner override logged (`override`). 3) *Predaj* → server snapshots `theoretical_qty`, computes variance; lines beyond `tolerance_qty` need a note; `log(count_submitted)`. 4) Incoming custodian taps *Potvrđujem stanje* → `witnessed_by` (nullable; unwitnessed counts are attention lines, not blocked), `log(count_witnessed)` (quiet). 5) Owner *Primijeni* → `count_adjust` (computed from the current theoretical at the count's `submitted_at`, so rounds that synced between submit and confirm are already in); confirm shows the delta between submitted and current theoretical (late rounds); `log(count_confirmed)` resolves the `count_submitted` entry. Under 5 minutes.

**F10 — Zatvori smjenu (shift close).** The venue's shift is *closed* (zatvorena) by the šanker or owner; each waiter *finishes* (završava) his own shift inside it — F13 — and there is exactly one end-of-shift path per waiter, the settlement below. 1) Blocked while any tab is `open`; the list offers *Naplati* / *Nije plaćeno* / *Predaj kolegi*; remaining drafts on any phone must be locked or *Odbaci* (`log(draft_discarded)`, quiet). When the closer starts this step, `/api/tables/state` answers `shift_closing:true` and every member without a settlement sees the bottom bar "Emir zatvara smjenu — predaj gotovinu" on his next poll (S1). 2) **Per waiter = *Završi smjenu* (F13).** Blind, from any device holding his session — his own phone normally; a borrowed session on a colleague's phone or the shared device when his own died (§5): *Predajem gotovinu: ___ KM* → the request carries this device's `outbox_len` (must be 0; S9 blocks while this device's money outbox is non-empty) and the server inspects the heartbeat of every device that locked a round for this user in this shift: any fresh one (≤ `heartbeat_fresh_s`) with `pending_count > 0` → `409 PENDING_OUTBOX` ("Amarov telefon se javio prije 3 min, 2 neposlane"); stale ones → allowed with `unsent_at_settle` (their ids and last `pending_count`) and an attention line; when a stale phone revives, its rounds arrive as late (F3 step 5), the summary gets a new version and his entry a quiet `settlement_updated` child. The settle transaction inserts `waiter_settlements`, computes `summary_json` with `summarizeUser()` and writes `log(waiter_finished)` (F13). Then reveal with breakdown: "Očekivano 412,50 · na čekanju: storno 5,00 (Sto 3), nije plaćeno 24,00 (Sto 9)" and the verdict in words: "razlika −4,50 · u toleranciji (do 5,00)" or "razlika −12,00 · označeno za razgovor". Receiver: šanker/owner PIN (`log(settlement_accepted)`, resolving the *Završena smjena* entry); or *Predajem u kasu* (`self_sealed=1`, envelope in the safe) accepted by the owner remotely or next morning via `/settlements/:sid/accept` (the one permitted transition, §6); lone closer → `self_accepted=1`, attention "Amar sam predao 312,50 KM — provjeri kovertu". After settlement the user cannot lock or pay in this shift (`409 SETTLED`); *Sažetak* shows his categories with KM (F13); *Odjavi se* sets `left_at`. 3) *Isplate iz kase*: amount + reason + note → `cash_movements(type='payout', status='pending')` (`log(payout_requested)`, decision `log(payout_decided)` resolves it); pending payouts do not reduce expected cash; above `payout_owner_fen` (50 KM) the owner must approve now. 4) Custodian's closing *Brzi popis* (F9). If the shift has no submitted opening count (F1 step 3), close is refused with "Nema početnog popisa" unless the owner overrides (`log(override)` with `body.what='no_open_count'`, attention line); the closing variance of such a shift is listed "bez početnog popisa" and excluded from the custodian's 30-day per-custodian number, never from the per-item one. 5) Closer counts the drawer + envelopes → `cash_counted_fen`; `|counted − expected| > max(5 KM, 1 %)` requires `closing_note`. 6) Closer's PIN → `status='closed'`, `shift_summaries` v1 with `by_category_json` and `by_user_json`, `log(shift_closed)` mirrored to Telegram: the pazar message (promet, gotovina, kartica, gratis, storna, cash diff, manjak KM from the submitted closing count, "popis predan · 3 van tolerancije", top aroma, zatvoreno/zadnja narudžba) gains one line per waiter — "Amar 612,50 · predao 598,00 · u toleranciji" — counts and words, never the fen value of a person's diff (§8). A close more than `early_close_min` before `closing_time` is listed as `early_close` with the last lock time ("zatvoreno 01:32 · zadnja narudžba 01:10"). 7) Forgotten close: Telegram `shift_not_closed` at cutoff − 1 h; owner `force_close` (`closed_kind='forced'`, mandatory note, `log(shift_forced)` naming who has no settlement); missing settlements become "Nije predao gotovinu" with *Naknadna predaja* (a new settlement row on the closed shift — inserts are allowed; `log(settlement_late)`), which computes the same `summary_json` and posts the same entry, only later.

Expected cash, one function `expectedCash(tx, shiftId, userId?)`, evaluated at read time so every decision moves it: waiterExpected = own `float_out` + Σ own cash `payments.amount_fen` (reversals included, `shift_id` = this shift) + Σ remaining balances (§6, not totals) of tabs `unpaid AND pending_review AND unpaid_by = me` + Σ `amount_fen` of own `line_adjustments(kind='void', was_paid=0)` that are `pending`, or `rejected` on a tab that is `paid` (the drink was served and never collected — the requester holds that money until an approver decides) − Σ `amount_fen` of own `applied` voids with `was_paid=1` decided after own settlement in this shift (`decided_at > settlement.created_at`; no reversing row exists for these, F6 step 5). drawerExpected = openingFloat + Σ `float_in` − Σ approved `payout` − Σ `float_out` − Σ `refund`. venueExpected = drawerExpected + Σ waiterExpected. `tip_fen` never enters; `received_fen` is display only. Consequences worth stating: approving a pending void after the requester's settlement moves his derived diff by exactly `amount_fen` (was_paid=0: the pending term leaves; was_paid=1: the applied-after-settlement term enters), rejecting moves nothing; a pending void never lowers what the venue expects from anyone. `withinTolerance(diff_fen, promet_fen)` = `|diff| ≤ max(cash_tolerance_fen, promet × cash_tolerance_pct)` is the one function behind S9's verdict, the Smjena strip, the Dnevnik chip and the Telegram word.

**F11 — Pregled dana (owner review, 5 minutes).** 1) Telegram deep link, or the *Dnevnik* badge. 2) Header: pazar, gotovina/kartica, cash diff, card diff, manjak KM, bowls with g/bowl and coal/bowl. 3) *Zahtijeva pažnju*: pending voids/comps/payouts, unpaid and late-sync tabs (*Otpis* / *Naplatiti*, F5 step 5), uncovered payments, count lines over tolerance, unwitnessed counts, shifts without an opening count, waste cumulative, foreign-device approvals, `storno_then_pay` sequences, stale devices and offline gaps, clock skew, float override, early close, self-sealed settlements (*Prihvati*), unfilled swap requests (F14), "planirano, nema smjene" / "radio bez rasporeda" lines — *Odobri / Odbij / Bilješka / Razgovarano* one tap each. 4) `card_total_fen` from the terminal Z report, entered here by the owner, never by the closer; `card_diff_fen = card_total − Σ card payments`, non-zero needs a note. 5) *Uzeo iz kase* → `owner_pickup` (drives the next opening float, `log(pickup)`). 6) Per-waiter strip from the latest `by_user_json` (names joined at read), each row with the live tolerance chip and the waiter's *Napomena* inline; a tap opens the drill-down (F13). 7) "Nakon zatvaranja" block (post-close decisions and late rounds). 8) *Pregledano* (`log(shift_reviewed)`).

**F12 — Razgovor (group chat, R1 + R4).** Chat is a route, never an overlay; it makes no sound, shows no toast and never touches an order draft (§10 invariant 10).

*(a) Channels and access.* Three fixed rows per venue: `svi`, `konobari`, `admini`. Access is one pure function `canSee(role, kind)` in `shared/chat.ts`, used by every route and test:

| `users.role` | Svi | Konobari | Admini |
|---|---|---|---|
| owner | yes | no | yes |
| manager (Phase 5) | yes | no | no |
| bartender | yes | yes | no |
| waiter | yes | yes | no |

The šanker is staff: he pours and approves, but he stands on the floor every night with the waiters, and "staff only" (R1) means the people the owner manages; a supervisor in *Konobari* is exactly what R1 forbids (§15 Q18 if the owner disagrees — a one-line change). The server enforces the matrix on every read and write (`403 CHANNEL_FORBIDDEN`), on `since` (messages from forbidden channels are never selected), on `reply_to_id` (`422 REPLY_CROSS_CHANNEL` — a quoted first line must not carry *Konobari* text into *Svi*) and on image URLs (c). The client hides what it cannot see; the test suite proves the server does too. **Membership is visible**: the S16 header lists every active user the matrix admits ("Članovi 5 · Amar, Lejla, Dino, Emir (šanker), Haris"), and *Konobari* receives a system line for every `user_changed` event that could put someone new in the room — "Lejla dodana (konobar) · Haris", "PIN za Amara resetovan · Haris", "Amar deaktiviran" — so the people in the channel learn who joined and who can now log in as whom. *Pravila*: "Vlasnik ne pravi naloge koje sam koristi."

**Honesty note, verbatim in *Pravila* (S12, "Šta aplikacija bilježi"):** "Vlasnik u aplikaciji ne vidi kanal *Konobari* i ne može ga otvoriti. Poruke su ipak zapisane u bazi na serveru, kao i sve ostalo; bazu mogu otvoriti vlasnik i Vedran. Kanal nije tajan — samo nije na vlasnikovom ekranu. Ne pišite ništa što ne biste rekli naglas. Poruke se brišu nakon 12 mjeseci, slike nakon 90 dana; kopije u sigurnosnim kopijama žive još do 60 dana. Obrisana slika nestaje sa servera odmah, sa telefona do sat vremena kasnije; original ostaje u galeriji onome ko ju je slikao. Ko je šta pročitao aplikacija pamti samo da bi brojala nepročitane poruke — niko to ne vidi na ekranu." The owner's commitment not to open the file is a *Pravila* rule, not code, and the plan says so (§15 Q20).

*(b) Messages.* Text ≤ 2000 chars, one optional image with caption, `reply_to_id` renders the quoted first line, no edits (fix a message by deleting and resending), no @mentions or reactions in v1. `seq` is venue-wide, assigned inside the insert transaction. Sending: `POST /api/chat/messages {client_id, channel_id, kind, body?, upload_id?, reply_to_id?, client_created_at}` → `{id, seq, created_at}`; `409 ALREADY_APPLIED` on replay, `422 BODY_EMPTY|BODY_TOO_LONG|UPLOAD_NOT_YOURS|REPLY_CROSS_CHANNEL`, `403 CHANNEL_FORBIDDEN|MUTED`; 10/min per device. **Money never enters *Svi* or *Konobari*.** By the system: `chat.postSystem(tx, venueId, kind, system_key, text, payload)` in `server/services/chat.ts` rejects any `*_fen` payload outside *Admini* (tested) — a waiter's settlement is a Dnevnik entry for admins, not a chat line. By people: the composer in *Svi*/*Konobari* shows a confirm sheet "Iznosi kolega ne idu u Svi — pošalji u Admini?" with *Ipak pošalji* when the body matches `/\d+[,.]\d{2}\s*KM/` or `(očekivano|manjak|razlika|predao)`; *Ipak pošalji* writes a quiet `chat_money_warned` entry. Owner sessions get *Slikaj* only in *Svi* — no *Galerija*, because screenshots of `/admin` live in the gallery and a camera photo of a screen is deliberate. *Pravila*, verbatim: "Ničiji pazar, manjak ili razlika ne ide u Svi ni u Konobare — ni kao slika."

*(c) Images (R4).* **Capture:** `<input type="file" accept="image/*" capture="environment">` for *Slikaj* and a second input without `capture` for *Galerija*; the native camera opens, returns one file, needs no permission dialog of our own (`getUserMedia` rejected, §17). iOS opens the camera directly with `capture` and shows the Take Photo / Library sheet without it, so two buttons behave the same on both platforms. **One decode path** in `app/utils/image.ts`: `URL.createObjectURL(file)` → `<img>` (browsers honour EXIF orientation on decode) → canvas with the longest edge ≤ `maxEdge` — `downscale(file, {maxEdge, quality})` takes 1280 px / 0.75 for `kind='chat'` (100–180 KB typical) and 1600 px / 0.85 for `kind='delivery'` (F8, the resolution the delivery note needs for OCR) → `toBlob('image/jpeg', quality)`; re-encoding drops EXIF (GPS, device) — a privacy plus stated in *Pravila*. A file the canvas cannot decode (HEIC on an odd Safari) gets "Ovaj format ne radi — slikaj iz aplikacije". No thumbnails: the same file is rendered at 320 px with CSS, `loading="lazy"`, and a `width/height` placeholder so the list never jumps. **Upload route** `POST /api/uploads` (multipart `image`, `kind`): checks the JPEG magic bytes (`415 NOT_JPEG`), size ≤ 1.5 MB for `kind='chat'` and ≤ 2.5 MB for `kind='delivery'` (`413 IMAGE_TOO_BIG`; nginx allows 4 m), `kind='delivery'` only for owner sessions or the šanker when `bartender_can_receive_goods` (`422 KIND_FORBIDDEN`), per-user daily caps `upload_user_day_files` 25 / `upload_user_day_bytes` 30 MB (`413 USER_CAP`, "Dnevni limit slika — sutra opet"), and the venue's monthly cap on `kind='chat'` `chat_image_month_bytes` 200 MB (`413 STORAGE_CAP`, "Mjesečni limit slika je pun — javi vlasniku"); orphan uploads (no message yet) do not count toward the caps and have their file unlinked by `uploads_gc` after 60 minutes (§5); any cap that trips writes a quiet `chat_cap_hit` entry naming the user. Writes `/var/lib/sank/uploads/{kind}/YYYY/MM/{uuid}.jpg` (0600), inserts `uploads`, returns `{upload_id, width, height, bytes}`. **Serving, no public files:** `GET /api/uploads/:id` streams from disk only after: valid session, same venue, and either (owner and `kind='delivery'`) or the upload is referenced by a non-deleted `chat_messages` row in a channel the requester may see — otherwise `404`. Headers: `Content-Type: image/jpeg`, `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, `Content-Disposition: inline; filename="slika.jpg"`, `Cache-Control: private, max-age=3600`, and `Content-Security-Policy: default-src 'none'` on the `/api` origin — a polyglot JPEG/HTML must never run on the app origin. **Deletion:** a file is unlinked only when no non-deleted `chat_messages` row references the `upload_id` (a forward shares the upload, so the forwarded copy survives the author's delete — the escalation path must not die with the original); the reference count runs inside the delete transaction. **Retention:** chat images 90 days (nightly 05:40, file unlinked, `uploads.deleted_at` set, the message shows "Slika istekla"); delivery photos are evidence beside a posted delivery and follow the ledger. Worst case on disk: 90 days × 200 MB ≈ 600 MB on a 20–40 GB VPS. **Backup:** `uploads/delivery/` goes to R2 with the database (file list from the DB, §5); `uploads/chat/` stays on the VPS only — a dead server loses chat photos and *Pravila* says so.

*(d) Offline.* Unsent text and photos live in the chat store's own pending list (`chat:pending` in `idb-keyval`: `{client_id, channel_id, kind, body?, blob?, client_created_at}`), flushed oldest-first on `online`, `visibilitychange`, after every send and after each money-outbox flush, with `AbortSignal.timeout(8000)`; a `4xx` drops the entry with the toast "Poruka nije poslana", not the "Popravi ili odbaci" gate — chat is not money; an entry older than 24 h is dropped the same way. Photos are downscaled before queueing (a ≤ 300 KB `Blob` — `idb-keyval` stores Blobs), at most 5 pending images ("Sačuvaj sliku u galeriju, pošalji kad bude veze" beyond that), because the file input's capture is a temporary file that iOS never writes to Photos — a photo of the mess taken at 15:55 on dead Wi-Fi must not be lost. The bubble shows a clock and "čeka slanje" until polling returns the server row matched by `client_id`; chip copy "Nema veze — poruka se čuva". This list is never counted in `outbox_len`, the heartbeat, the sync chip, the logout gate or the settlement/count gates (§5). Pin edits are online only.

*(e) Reading, badges, notifications.* `GET /api/chat/since` has two modes: with no `cursor` (first open, wiped IndexedDB) it returns `channels[]` and the last 30 messages per visible channel plus `cursor = MAX(seq)`; with a cursor it returns `{cursor, channels:[{id, kind, name, unread, last_seq, preview, pinned_text, pinned_at, members[]}], messages[], has_more}` for `seq > cursor` (cap 200, oldest first), and answers `{reset:true}` when `MAX(seq) − cursor > 1000` so the client re-bootstraps. ETag = `${venue max seq}:${user max last_read_seq}`; `304` costs one index lookup. `GET /api/chat/messages?channel_id&before_seq&limit=50` pages backwards for "Učitaj starije". The cursor is persisted in `idb-keyval` (`chat:cursor`). **One timer**: the existing 15 s poll carries `chat_max_seq`; S16 additionally calls `since` on mount, after every own send and on `visibilitychange`. **Badges:** `unread` counts only `kind IN ('text','image')` — system lines are shown in the thread but never bump a badge, so the badge means "a person wrote something"; total unread as a count bubble on the avatar in the `/konobar` header (the avatar sheet lists *Razgovor · Raspored · Moja smjena · Završi smjenu* with their counts — S1 keeps its four header controls); per-channel counts on S15; capped at "99+". `POST /api/chat/read {channel_id, seq}` fires, debounced 1 s, when the newest message is rendered. No "seen by": it would hand the owner a per-person reading log ("Amar je vidio i nije odgovorio"), which §8 forbids in spirit. **Notifications:** in-app badge only; no sound or vibration by default (`users.chat_sound`, off). Telegram `chat_digest` for owners: one message when `max_seq` of a digest channel grew since the last digest and ≥ 30 min passed, at most `chat_digest_day_cap` 4 per day per owner, channels from `chat_digest_channels` (default *Admini* only; *Svi* opt-in in *Postavke*), 80 chars, no images, deep link, quiet hours as §11. *Konobari* is never mirrored anywhere. Staff get nothing pushed in v1 — chat is not for "sto 5 hoće žar"; that is the order flow.

*(f) Moderation.* Own text message: *Obriši* within `chat_delete_own_s` (900; `409 DELETE_WINDOW` after). Owners delete anything in *Svi* and *Admini* at any age ("Obrisao vlasnik · 22:41"), may *Utišaj* a user (`POST /api/chat/users/:id/mute {until}` → `users.chat_muted_until`, `403 MUTED` with "Vlasnik te utišao do 10:00", `log(chat_muted)`) and may *Obriši sve od Amara u zadnjih 30 min* (one transaction of soft-deletes). *Konobari*: text is deletable only by its author within 15 minutes; **images may be removed by any member at any age** (*Ukloni sliku* → soft-deleted with `deleted_by`, placeholder "Sliku uklonio Emir · 22:41", quiet `chat_image_removed` entry naming author and remover, no image), because a photo of a guest or a colleague's ID must not sit for 90 days where the data controller cannot reach it. Escalation: *Proslijedi u Svi* on any *Konobari* message and *Prijavi vlasniku* on any *Konobari* or *Svi* message = a forward into *Admini* on the reporter's behalf — the one staff → *Admini* path, so a waiter can flag a leak or an abuse without the owner's cooperation; owners *Proslijedi u Admini* from *Svi*. Forwarding creates a new message in the target channel with `forwarded_from_id`, body prefixed "↪ Amar (Konobari, 22:41): …", the same `upload_id` for images. A soft-deleted text keeps its body until retention (moderation evidence; the placeholder shows who and when, not the text). *Pravila*: "Šta napišeš u Konobarima kolega može proslijediti. Sliku u Konobarima može ukloniti svako; tekst samo autor. Slike samo šanka, robe i prostora — gosti nikad."

*(g) Pinned note and system messages.* `chat_channels.pinned_text` (≤ 500 chars) on every channel; the one that matters is *Svi*'s **"Za naručiti"**. Two ways to edit: the pin bar → textarea → *Sačuvaj* (`POST /api/chat/channels/:id/pin {text}`, the owner's tidy-up), or long-press a message → *Dodaj u "Za naručiti"* (`{append}`: the server appends the message's first line ≤ 80 chars as a new line — no lost update, no keyboard, 2 taps). Both write the column and a system message "Amar je izmijenio *Za naručiti*: led, limun, Coca-Cola 2 gajbe", so history keeps every version. An owner taps *Naručeno ✓* → the note is cleared and a system line says "Haris: naručeno". System messages come only from `postSystem`, inside the caller's transaction, centred and grey, text plus an optional `system_payload_json.link {label, route}` rendered as one 48 px secondary button ("Raspored →") — never an action of its own; chat never calls roster or shift routes. Keys share the names of the log kinds they accompany: `pin_changed`, `roster_published`, `roster_changed` (one line per owner per week per business day — the first post-publish edit of the day posts "Haris mijenja raspored 14.–20.09. — Raspored →", later edits that day post nothing, F14), `swap_requested` (*Konobari*), `swap_accepted` (*Svi*, outcome only, no reason, no note — the identical line whether the request's reason was `zamjena` or `bolest`; there is no separate key for a sickness cover, because a distinct key would itself be the reason), `user_changed` (*Konobari*), `rules_published` (*Svi*), Phase 5 `stock_below_par` (*Admini*).

*(h) Tests (vitest, `:memory:` with migrations + triggers).* Access matrix for every role × channel × {since, post, read, pin, forward}; `since` never returns a *Konobari* row to an owner; a `manager` gets *Svi* only. Replay a send twice → one row; `seq` monotonic over 200 inserts in three channels. One-transition: only `deleted_at/deleted_by` (and once `redacted_at/body`) may change; UPDATE of `body` and DELETE throw. Delete rules: own within 900 s ok, after → 409; owner in *Svi* at any age; owner in *Konobari* → 403 even with a forged channel id; any member removes a *Konobari* image at any age, but not text. Forward from *Konobari* to *Svi*: new row with `forwarded_from_id`; the image is served to an owner after and was `404` before; deleting the original after the forward keeps the file. `reply_to_id` from another channel → 422. A `bolest` swap request accepted → the *Svi* row's `body` and `system_payload_json` contain neither "bolest" nor "bolovanje". Upload: PNG bytes with a `.jpg` name → 415; 1.6 MB with `kind='chat'` → 413, 2.4 MB with `kind='delivery'` → 201; waiter with `kind='delivery'` → 422; 26th file of the day → 413 `USER_CAP`; 201 MB in the month → 413 `STORAGE_CAP`; orphan at 61 min → file gone, `uploads.deleted_at` set, row kept; a `discarded` scan at 61 min → file gone, both rows kept; a `parsed` scan at 61 min → file kept; file mode 0600. Retention: chat image at 91 days → file gone, `deleted_at` set, message kept; delivery photo at 400 days untouched. `since` with no cursor returns ≤ 30 per channel; catch-up caps at 200 with `has_more`; a cursor 1001 behind gets `reset`; unread counts exclude system rows and drop after `read`; ETag `304` when nothing changed. `postSystem` with a `*_fen` payload into *Svi* throws; a pin edit writes exactly one system message; `{append}` from two devices keeps both lines. One queued chat entry and zero money entries → settle `200` and heartbeat `pending=0`. Staff session on `/api/chat/users/:id/mute` → 403. The schema test already fails if a chat table lacks `venue_id`; the Phase 5 multi-venue test adds `since` and uploads to its leak list.

*(b) Završi smjenu — the waiter's lock-in.* **One end-of-shift, one endpoint.** S9 is titled *Završi smjenu*; its steps are unchanged (open tables and drafts → blind *Predajem gotovinu* → receiver PIN or *Predajem u kasu* → reveal with the tolerance verdict → *Sažetak* → *Odjavi se*); `POST /api/shifts/:id/settle` is the lock-in; the `409 SETTLED` that follows is what "locked in" means. Extra taps for the waiter: **0**. The verb is *završiti* (waiter) vs *zatvoriti* (closer) — *zaključiti* stays reserved for locking a round, the most tapped word in the app (§17). **Where the button is:** in the avatar sheet always (2 taps), and pinned as a 56 px bottom bar on S1 — *Završi smjenu · predaj gotovinu* — whenever the shift is open, the waiter has 0 open tabs, 0 drafts and an empty money outbox, and either `now > closing_time − 2 h` or his last lock is > 45 min old (1 tap, impossible to miss at 00:10); the same bar appears with "Emir zatvara smjenu — predaj gotovinu" when the closer starts F10 (`shift_closing:true`). No sound, no modal. *Pravila*: "Predaješ gotovinu kad završiš, ne prije."

**The settle transaction** (F10 step 2) inserts `waiter_settlements`, then calls `summarizeUser(tx, shiftId, userId, now)`:
- `promet_fen` = Σ `charged_fen` of lines in orders with `locked_by = user` in this shift − Σ `amount_fen` of `applied` voids on them; `unpaid_fen` separately (sold, not collected).
- `by_category[] = {category_id, count, fen, void_count, void_fen, comp_count, comp_fen}` by `products.category_id` (free text → *Ostalo*, none → *Bez kategorije*); `fen` is net of applied voids; `count` = Σ `qty` excluding top-ups (`parent_line_id IS NOT NULL`, the 0 KM *Dodatni žar*), a mixed bowl is one bowl via `countBowls()` in `shared/bowls.ts` (moved here in Phase 2; the §9 *Nargila* report imports the same function); top-ups show as "+ žar 21" in the drill-down header; gratis lines count in their category with `fen` 0 and are listed separately at `unit_price_fen`.
- `storno` (count, fen, of which pending), `gratis` (count, value), `tabs`, `rounds`, `cash_fen` / `card_fen` = own `payments` by `paid_by` (reversals included), `expected_fen` = `expectedCash(tx, shift, user)` at that instant, `declared_fen`, `tolerance_fen`, `within_tolerance` (from `withinTolerance()`, F10), `joined_at` → `settled_at`, `hours`.
The object is stored as `waiter_settlements.summary_json` (set at insert; the one-transition trigger is untouched), returned by the route so S9 *Sažetak* needs no second call, and placed in `body_json` of the `waiter_finished` entry. **The diff is never stored as a number in a title or a message**: `body_json` holds `declared_fen` and `expected_at_declare_fen`; the Dnevnik card, the Smjena strip and the drill-down header render a live chip from `expectedCash(now)` — "u toleranciji" when `withinTolerance`, otherwise "razlika −12,00 · sada −7,00 (storno odobren 09:41)" — yellow only outside tolerance, red only after 3-in-30 (§8). Anything that later touches the settlement (a post-settlement void decision, late rounds from a revived phone) writes a quiet `settlement_updated` child with `resolves_id` so the card says "nakon predaje: storno odobren −3,00" or "+2 ture kasnije · promet sada 650,00" while `summary_json` stays byte-identical. Promet is attributed to the **locker**, cash to the **payer**; after a table handover one waiter's promet exceeds his cash, and both sit on the same line to pre-empt the accusation.

**Whole shift at close.** `shift_summaries` v1 carries `by_category_json` (venue) and `by_user_json` (every `shift_members` user, šanker included, each with its own `by_category`), ids and integers only; every post-close decision writes the next version; the Smjena per-waiter strip reads the latest version for closed shifts and computes live for the open one. Invariant: Σ `by_user.promet_fen` = Σ `by_category.fen` = `promet_fen`.

**Drill-down (R3: "34 nargile" is clickable).** One component, `LinesList.vue` (props `rows`, `showWaiter`), one two-line row layout on phone and laptop — "21:47 · Sto 7 · Nargila jabuka + menta · 15,00 · naplaćeno" — with a header chip row that switches `kat` in place and a footer whose sums equal the header. **F14 — Raspored & zamjena (R5).**

*(a) Model in one paragraph.* A **template** (`shift_templates`) is a named time span the owner defines: seed *Dnevna* 08–16 and *Večernja* 16–01; more are an insert (*Vikend večernja* 18–03). The **grid** is one week (pon → ned) × templates; a cell is the people assigned to that template on that day. A week is a `roster_weeks` row that is *Nacrt* until the owner taps *Objavi raspored*; after that every edit is live immediately and announced — no second publish, no draft/published pair of tables. `work_date` is the local calendar date the shift starts, always equal to the `shifts.business_date` it is worked in, which makes the planned-vs-worked join a plain equality. Rows are mutable and every write goes through `services/roster.ts`, which writes `log()` with before/after in the same transaction (§6); the fairness-relevant history is rows anyway — a swap never overwrites `user_id` (the giver's row becomes `swapped` — or keeps `sick` when the request's reason was `bolest`, (c) — and the taker gets a new `origin='swap'` row), a post-publish removal is `status='removed'` with a note, never a delete; hard `DELETE` only while the week is unpublished. Planned hours are nominal, `(end − start) mod 24` from the snapshotted `HH:MM` — no timezone math, no DST-exact rostering (§17); worked hours are UTC differences from `shift_members`.

*(b) Owner builds next week.* Raspored → *Sljedeća sedmica* (empty, "Nacrt") → *Kopiraj prošlu sedmicu* copies rows with `origin ≠ 'swap' AND status ≠ 'removed'` (the regular people, not one-off covers), skipping `users.active=0` (`409 WEEK_NOT_EMPTY` if the week has rows). Tap a cell → avatar picker of active staff (waiters and bartenders, by name; people already working that day carry a small "Dnevna" tag) → tap a person: **2 taps**, the picker stays open for a second person; a non-overlapping second template on the same day is `409 DOUBLE_SHIFT` unless the body carries `force_double:true`, which the sheet *Dupla smjena — svejedno dodaj* sets on the retry — asked once, **3 taps** worst case; an overlapping one is `409 OVERLAP` with no override. Remove: tap chip → *Ukloni* (2). *Objavi raspored* → `published_at`, `log(roster_published)`, system line in *Svi* "Raspored za 14.09.–20.09. je objavljen — Raspored →". Edits before publish post nothing; after publish every edit writes a quiet `roster_changed` entry and *Svi* gets at most one line per owner per business day ("Haris mijenja raspored 14.–20.09. — Raspored →"), so a Friday of six fixes is one line and the waiters' badge stays honest (system lines never bump it anyway, F12 e). **System lines never carry a note or a reason**; the owner's note on a removal is for *Dnevnik* only. A typical week: copy + publish = 3 taps; four fixes ≈ 8–12 taps.

*(c) Waiter — Raspored (S17).* Staff see only published weeks, and of colleagues' rows only who works when: the staff projection of `GET /api/roster` strips `note`, `updated_by`, `swap_request_id`, and maps a colleague's `sick|absent|removed` row to a hole; own rows keep full status. *Pravila*: "Bolovanje vidi samo vlasnik." Tap own shift → sheet: **Traži zamjenu** → optional colleague (an offer) or nobody (open to all staff) → reason chip *zamjena* / *bolest* → optional note → one `POST /api/roster/swaps` → `pending`; **`reason='bolest'` sets the assignment `status='sick'` in the same transaction — the one way a waiter's sickness enters the roster** (the row returns to `planned` only when the requester withdraws the request with *Povuci* or the owner clears it); a system line in *Konobari* "Amar traži zamjenu · pet 18.09. Večernja 16–01 — Raspored →" (open requests) or a card on the named colleague's S17; `reason='bolest'` also sends Telegram `roster_sick` to owners and writes `roster_sick` — the reason is shown on `/admin` *Zamjene* and in Telegram only, never in chat: the *Konobari* line and the later *Svi* line read exactly as for `zamjena`. **Preuzimam** (the named colleague, or anyone for an open request) on S17 → `POST /:id/accept` → one transaction, after the taker passes the same overlap/double-shift rules (`409 DOUBLE_SHIFT` → the sheet *Dupla smjena — svejedno preuzmi* retries with `force_double:true`): giver row `swapped` (a `sick` giver row stays `sick`, so *Sati* counts the sick day and the grid keeps the struck-through chip), taker row `planned/origin='swap'/swap_request_id`, request `accepted` with `decided_by = taker`, `log(swap_accepted)` resolving `swap_requested`, *Svi* line "Zamjena · pet 18.09. Večernja — Dino umjesto Amara" (identical for a `bolest` request). Everyone's next 15 s poll shows the new grid. Requester *Povuci* → `cancelled`; a named colleague *Odbij* → `declined`, the requester's card says so; both quiet entries. A mutual swap is two requests. **No owner confirmation in v1**: the owner sees every outcome in *Svi* and *Dnevnik* and can edit the cell — that is the veto; `swap_needs_owner` exists as a setting for Phase 5, unimplemented (§15 Q21). Tap budget: avatar → Raspored → own shift → Traži zamjenu → reason → *Pošalji* = **5–6**; taking a shift: avatar → Raspored → *Preuzimam* = **3**. **Until Phase 3b lands** (the request lifecycle ships during the pilot, §13), *Pravila* carries the interim line "Zamjenu dogovori u Konobarima — vlasnik je upiše u raspored; bolestan? Javi vlasniku.": the owner's 2-tap cell edit is the swap and the owner marks the row *Bolestan* (e), so the first pilot weeks lose nothing but the self-service.

*(d) The owner sees an unfilled request.* A `pending` request is an attention item, not a *Konobari* secret: the owner's grid shows the cell with an amber chip "Amar traži zamjenu"; *Zahtijeva pažnju* lists "Nepopunjena smjena: pet 18.09. Večernja — Amar traži zamjenu · 2 dana"; Telegram `swap_unfilled` when the shift starts in < 24 h and again at < 4 h while still pending (dedupe per request and stage). Owner actions on the card: **Dodijeli** (avatar picker → assigns a taker and accepts in the same transaction, `decided_by = owner`, 3 taps; allowed up to 7 days after `work_date` as an acknowledgement, shown "dodijeljeno naknadno", so a Friday cover the owner learns of on Saturday still lands in the hours join) or *Odbij*. The owner editing a cell with a live request cancels it (note "vlasnik promijenio ćeliju") and the requester's card says so. Staff `accept` on `work_date < today` → `409 ROSTER_LOCKED`; same-day accepts are always allowed, also after `start_time` (the row is snapshotted with a note "potvrđeno nakon početka").

*(e) Absence and past dates.* After the day, the owner marks *Nije došao* (`planned → absent`, `log(roster_absent)`, important) or clears it back to `planned`; past-date `PATCH` allows only `planned ↔ absent` and `planned → sick` for owners, never `removed` (`422 PAST_LOCKED`) — an admin cannot retroactively remove a person from the night stock went missing; an owner may also set `planned → sick` on a future row (the waiter phoned instead of tapping). Staff never `PATCH` a status (`403`): a waiter's sickness enters only through *Traži zamjenu* with reason `bolest` (c), so there is one path and one count. `ROSTER_LOCKED` for create/delete when `work_date < today`. The waiter attaches a *Napomena* (`staff_notes`) to his own row like any other row (§8). Nothing is auto-marked. The *Sati* page shows an "izmijenjeno 10.09. · Haris" chip on rows with a `roster_changed` entry after `work_date`.

*(f) Constraints.* Hard: the partial unique index (same person twice in one cell); `409 OVERLAP` for two overlapping templates on one date (also for a taker); `409 ROSTER_LOCKED` as above; staff touch only their own rows; deactivating a user turns his future `planned` rows into `removed` (note "deaktiviran") and cancels his live requests. Soft (confirm once, never blocked): a second non-overlapping template on one day. No `min_staff`, no rest-hours logic, no consecutive-days warning, no holiday calendar in v1 — an empty cell is visible enough (§17).

*(g) Planned vs worked (Phase 3b).* `GET /api/roster/hours?month=` aggregates `shift_members` first — `SELECT user_id, business_date, MIN(joined_at), MAX(left_at), MAX(left_at_source='auto') … GROUP BY user_id, business_date` (a date can have two `shifts` rows after an early close, F1 step 5, and a person two assignments) — then joins both ways: each assignment gets `planned_h`, `first_action = joined_at`, `left_at`, `worked_h` (shown once per user and date), `late_min = joined_at − (work_date + start_time in Europe/Sarajevo)` above `roster_late_grace_min`, `early_leave_min` (F13), `no_shift_row` when a `planned` row has no member row; and every member row without a `planned|swapped→taker` row becomes an **`unplanned`** row ("Radio bez rasporeda: Dino · pet 18.09. · prva tura 16:12") — the person who *was* there when stock went missing but is not on the plan. A `pending` request on a planned row renders "zamjena čekala potvrdu: Dino" instead of a bare flag. Caveat printed on the page: the first action is not the arrival, so "prva tura 16:40 (+40 min)" is evidence for a conversation, never a flag (§8). Per person per month: planned shifts and hours (the pay basis — most cafés pay a *dnevnica* per shift, §15 Q22), worked hours as evidence, sick days (= rows with `status='sick'`, whichever path set it), absences, swaps given/taken; `sati.csv` joins *Izvoz*. The waiter sees only his own rows in S11 *Moji sati*, which now shows both columns. The nightly 05:30 task adds attention lines "Planirano, nema smjene: Amar · Večernja 18.09." (for `planned` rows only — `sick`, `absent`, `swapped` and `removed` rows are skipped) and "Radio bez rasporeda: Dino" with matching entries.

*(h) Screens.* **S17 Raspored** (`/konobar/raspored`, from the avatar sheet; the avatar count includes offers and requests awaiting me). Segments *Ova sedmica* | *Sljedeća*; a card at the top for offers to me and my own requests ("Traži se zamjena · pet 18.09." with *Povuci*); seven day rows "pon 14.09." listing templates with initials, my shifts in the accent colour with the time; an unpublished next week says "Raspored za sljedeću sedmicu još nije objavljen." Last response cached in `idb-keyval` (`roster:last`, the staff projection only) so the screen opens offline; every write is online-only and shows "Nema veze" disabled, like *Premjesti sto* — no queue, because a swap is not urgent and idempotency here would need server-side conflict rules for nothing. **Owner** (`/admin/raspored`, phone under *Više*, laptop nav item). Laptop: templates × days grid with sticky headers, the same picker as a popover, 2 clicks. *(i) Tests (`services/roster.ts` against `:memory:` + triggers).* Same person twice in a cell → unique error; overlapping templates → `409 OVERLAP`; non-overlapping second template → `409 DOUBLE_SHIFT` without `force_double`, row with it. ## 8. Anti-theft & accountability rules

Stance: every KM and gram enters once at the source; every number is derived; patterns over shifts are flagged, never a single act; the person sees every per-person number first; thresholds are published on *Pravila*.

**Enforced (the database or a transaction makes it impossible, or forces a named approver):**
1. Append-only and one-transition triggers (§6) on every ledger, `log_entries` and `chat_messages` included; statuses recomputed server-side; closed shifts and paid tabs frozen.
2. No price, discount or amount from a phone: Zod rejects `charged_fen`/`amount` fields in order bodies; prices copied server-side at lock; free-text products carry fixed prices (seed "Ostalo 2 / 5 / 10 KM"); no discounts (`promo` comps cover them); price changes write `price_history` + `log_entries`.
3. Server time on every row; `shift_id` from the open shift; `business_date` computed once; phone clocks adjusted by measured skew.
4. Every event carries `user_id` and `device_id`; sessions bound to enrolled devices; borrowed sessions labelled; a lock from a settled user's device under someone else's session is an event.
5. Stock deduction in the lock transaction; re-added only by an `applied` void with `restock=1`; comps never restock; walk-outs are unpaid tabs, not voids.
6. **Pending does not reduce expected.** A pending void keeps its amount in the requester's expected cash (F10 term), a pending unpaid tab keeps its balance in the marker's, a pending comp or payout changes nothing; stock moves only on `applied`. The person holds the money until an approver decides. This one rule removes the incentive for post-payment voids and for "dobavljač leda 60 KM".
7. Blind cash and blind counts: `expected` only after `declared`/`counted`; `GET /api/me/shift` has no `*_fen` fields before own settlement (category counts yes, category sums no); S1 keeps per-table totals (needed to serve — a documented soft leak).
8. Owner approval only from the owner's own device; bartender approval on foreign devices flagged, and every bartender approval time-boxed (≤ 900 s since lock, also in his own queue); `owner_guest` only by the owner.
9. Waste: single event above 10 KM, own cumulative above 20 KM per shift, or any `is_spot` item → approver PIN.
10. **Fairness contract (product rules).** *Pravila* (`rules` table, versioned) is published before the first shift and every change is a `log_entries` row and a *Svi* notice; staff confirm each version once (`users.rules_ack_at`). The word is *odstupanje*; yellow means "označeno za razgovor", red only after three flagged shifts in 30 days; the waiter attaches a *Napomena* to any of his own rows; per-person baselines and deviation flags exist only with ≥ 10 shifts; no leaderboard; no colleague money in `/konobar` and none in *Svi* or *Konobari* (F12 b); `cash_custody='per_waiter'` and a named stock custodian so nobody pays another's manjak; every flag gets an outcome (*Razgovarano · OK / opomena / greška aplikacije*; `log(flag_outcome)` with `ref_type='flag'` → the `alert_events` row) — an OK outcome drops out of the 3-in-30 count and the waiter sees it on the same row. **Chat and log rules:** *Konobari* is not readable by owners in the app, and the honesty note (F12 a) says plainly that the database holds it and who can open the file; the owner's promise not to is a *Pravila* line, not code; there are no read receipts and no per-person chat statistics, so chat never becomes an accusation tool; *Dnevnik* is admin-only and its per-person entries carry counts, promet and the tolerance word, with the live diff computed at read time like everywhere else; photos are of the bar, the goods and the room — "gosti nikad" — any *Konobari* member can remove an image, the owner can remove anything in *Svi*, EXIF is stripped, chat photos live 90 days and never leave the VPS; sickness and absence are visible to owners only. Staff get things for themselves: *Moji sati* per shift and per month (planned and worked side by side once the roster exists; `joined_at`→`left_at`, forced close sets `left_at = closed_at` with `source='auto'`), *Raspored* (S17), a *Moji podaci* export (own messages included) with the list of own sessions, and a plain "Šta aplikacija bilježi" section in *Pravila* (fields listed, who sees what, no location/contacts, camera only when you tap *Slikaj*; retention 24 months for device and lockout rows, 12 months for chat text, 90 days for chat photos — the tasks are Phase 5 and Phase 3b; the Telegram mirror to owners of admin-channel excerpts and of per-waiter promet lines; the roster with sickness and absence visible to owners; `chat_reads` existing for badges and shown to nobody; the owner's drill-down of every line a waiter locked with device and arrival times). Reviewer-reported, not re-verified: BiH's new personal-data law (Sl. glasnik 12/25) has applied since October 2025 and follows GDPR; whether a small café needs more than a notice is an assumption. The venue-level "Zadnjih 20 stavki" feed stays on *Uživo* (mystery-guest check); per-person activity feeds do not exist — *Dnevnik* is per event and admin-only, and its *Osoba* filter is for "ko je ovo uradio?", not for a waiter's timeline. `bonus_pool_fen` (shared honesty bonus when monthly cash variance is within ±0.5 % and spot-stock variance within 2 % of cost) is announced on *Pravila* as planned and revisited after two months of trusted counts.

## 9. Stock system

**Recipes (normativ).** A `product` is what the waiter taps; a `stock_item` is what sits on the shelf; `recipe_lines` connect them. The 1:1 case is `products.sells_stock_item_id` and covers most of the menu with zero effort. Recipes cover espresso (7 g kafa + 5 g šećer as a `potrosni` item), gin tonik (30 ml gin + 1 kom tonik), and shisha: `kind='shisha'` products carry `shisha_grams` and `coal_pcs` (default 3); flavours resolve at lock from `order_line_flavours`, grams split equally. **Measured, not assumed:** at onboarding the owner packs 10 bowls per product on the scale; `shisha_grams` is that mean, `shisha_grams_measured_at` set, and the g/bowl band is ±15 %. "Dodatni žar" = 0 KM, recipe 2 kom coal; "Nova lula" = charged, own grams. Products with neither recipe nor 1:1 item sit in *Bez normativa*. Recipe edits affect only future rounds.

**Units and costs.** `base_unit ∈ kom|g|ml`; `pack_qty` converts (gajba 24, tin 250 g, coal box 64/72 — owner confirms). Screens show "paketa + komada" and store base units. Every stock item gets a mandatory `last_cost_fen` at onboarding (purchase price per pack, converted), so `opening` movements carry cost and `manjak_km` is real from day one; items with `avg_cost_fen = 0` appear in *Bez cijene* and count lines show "−1 kom · cijena?" in amber. Deliveries update the moving average (`new_avg = (on_hand×avg + qty×cost)/(on_hand+qty)`, or `cost` if `on_hand ≤ 0`).

**Movements.** `stock_movements` is the only truth; on hand = `SUM(qty_delta)` on every page (Stanje šanka, COGS, 30-day cumulatives, theoretical). Sales inside the lock transaction with `unit_cost_fen` from `avg_cost_fen` and `occurred_at = client_created_at_adj`. Applied `restock=1` voids write `sale_storno` per component. **Late sync after a confirmed count:** when a `sale` or `sale_storno` row is inserted with `occurred_at ≤ submitted_at` of a confirmed count for that (location, item), the same transaction inserts a `late_sync` row with `qty_delta = −row.qty_delta`, `unit_cost_fen` copied, `ref_type='stock_movement'`, `ref_id` = that row, `occurred_at` = the row's, note "kasno sinhronizovano · popis 07.09. 03:10". The confirmed count already absorbed that bottle as `count_adjust`, so the offset keeps `SUM(qty_delta)` from deducting it twice; the sale row still counts in COGS, and the count's manjak is reported as `variance − Σ late_sync` with the round listed under *Kasno sinhronizovano* ("objašnjava manjak 1 kom · 1,80 KM"). Waste explicit with reason (`log(waste_logged)`). Counts adjust only when the owner confirms. `correction` owner-only with note (`log(stock_corrected)`). Stock may go negative — a sale is never blocked; *U minusu* lists such items with "zadnji prijem prije 9 dana". *U minusu*, *Bez normativa*, *Bez cijene* are nag lists whose purpose is to make sloppy entry visibly expensive.

**Counts.** Spot list (`is_spot`, ~20 items): every spirit bottle, beer, top sodas, energy drinks, cigarettes, open tobacco tins (weighed), coal, coffee (weighed), sugar (weighed). Closing count by the custodian at every close; opening count by the same list at every open (F1) so the shift is bracketed and the variance belongs to one custodian — a close without one needs an owner override (F10 step 4). *Inventura* (`kind='full'`) is the same screen with the filter off, monthly, by the owner; quarter-tin estimates are allowed there only. Tolerances: sealed bottles 0, open spirits 30 ml, open tobacco 5 g, coffee 20 g, coal 2 kom. Theoretical at time T = last confirmed count + Σ movements with `occurred_at` in `(last.submitted_at, T]`; a late sale with earlier `occurred_at` is excluded (and, once the count is confirmed, offset by its `late_sync` row) and listed under *Kasno sinhronizovano* with its KM. Variance is reported per custodian (30-day, bracketed shifts only) and per item (30-day cumulative — one bottle a week is invisible in a shift and obvious in a month).

**Variance and cost.** `manjak_km = variance_qty × unit_cost_fen`; COGS per shift = Σ(−qty_delta × cost) over sale/storno rows; "stvarni trošak" adds waste, `count_adjust` and `late_sync` rows — the gap is the leakage number. Gross margin per product on the menu page once costs exist; margin dashboards Phase 5. Reorder lists (`par_qty`) Phase 5.

**Nargila — the owner's formula (monthly, owner's rule 3).** Tobacco is a `stock_item` per flavour with `base_unit='g'`, sealed packs converted by `pack_qty` (50 / 200 / 250 / 1000 g), open packs weighed. The venue norm `grams_per_bowl_default` is 20 g, so 1 kg = 50 bowls; a mixed bowl splits the norm across its flavours (2 aromas = 10 g + 10 g). The *Nargila* report (Roba → Nargila, presets *Ovaj mjesec · Prošli mjesec · Prilagođeno*) shows per flavour and in total: `početno_g` (the closing weight of the previous period — nothing resets, leftover carries over), `primljeno_g` (Σ `delivery` movements in the period, received on any day), `završno_g` (the last confirmed count in the period: the owner's monthly *inventura*, sealed packs counted, open packs weighed), `potrošeno_g = početno + primljeno − završno`, `očekivano_lula = potrošeno_g ÷ 20`, `prodano_lula` (Σ locked shisha lines through the shared `countBowls()` of `shared/bowls.ts` — the same function the per-waiter summary uses, F13; a mixed bowl counts as one bowl with its grams split per flavour, top-ups are not bowls), and `razlika = očekivano − prodano` as *lule bez narudžbe*, also in KM at the bowl price. The owner's own example is the acceptance test: 15 kg received, 4 kg left → 11 kg used → 550 expected bowls; 500 locked → 50 bowls (≈ 750 KM at 15 KM) unexplained, and the 4 kg is next month's `početno_g`. Per-product measured `shisha_grams` (the 10-bowl weighing) still drives the per-shift band (±15 %); the monthly report uses the 20 g norm because that is the number the owner reasons with, and shows *po normi 20 g* and *po izmjerenom* side by side when they differ. Coal is reported the same way in pieces (`žar po luli`).

**Kategorije — nabavka vs prodaja (owner's rule 4).** One list of `categories` serves both products and stock items (`products.category_id`, `stock_items.category_id`; Vedran sends the real list and mapping, §15 Q15 — the seed uses a placeholder such as Kafa · Bezalkoholna pića · Energetska · Nargila · Hrana · Potrošni). The *Kategorije* report (Roba → Kategorije, same presets) shows per category: **Nabavka** = Σ cost of its stock items' `delivery` (plus `correction`, minus `return_supplier`) movements — money spent on ordering in the period; **Prodaja** = Σ `charged_fen` of its products' locked lines − applied voids (the same grouping as `by_category_json`, so a month of shift summaries reconciles to this column); **Utrošak (COGS)** = Σ cost of its `sale` / `sale_storno` movements; **Bruto marža** = Prodaja − Utrošak, and as a percentage; **Otpis / manjak** = waste + `count_adjust` + `late_sync` cost; and **Prodaja − Nabavka**, the cash view the owner asked for, with a footnote that stocking up (buying 15 kg of tobacco while selling 11 kg) makes it swing — the margin is the fair number, the cash view is the honest one, both are shown. Coal and tobacco both roll into *Nargila*; sugar, cups and napkins into *Potrošni* or *Kafa* as the owner decides. Items and products without a category sit in *Bez kategorije* so the report is never silently incomplete. `kategorije.csv` exports the same table.

**Priprema (Phase 1).** `GET /api/prep` lists locked lines not yet marked `prepared` (`order_events(type='prepared')`, one tap) for the šanker's shared or personal device; house rule "no ticket, no drink; no lit coal for an unlocked bowl". On nights without a šanker it does nothing, and the weighed count carries the load.

**Onboarding.** Venue 1 is seeded by Vedran and the owner in one evening from the BiH lounge template (~10 categories, ~70 products with default recipes, brands, flavours, coal, the spot list, tables, zones, note chips, the three chat channels, the two shift templates), then *Početno stanje* with cost per item writes `opening` movements (`ref_type='venue_setup'`; untouched items start at 0, flagged "nije prebrojano"), then 10-bowl weighings per shisha product. A second `venues` row "Vježba" with the same template is seeded for training; its enrol codes bind devices to it, S0 shows a red VJEŽBA ribbon, and the owner's `/admin` switches between the two — the first multi-venue test for free. The self-service *Postavljanje lokala* wizard is Phase 5.

## 13. Roadmap

Effort in hours for Vedran planning with Fable, Opus implementing, Vedran reviewing every PR and debugging on real phones; ~30 % of each phase is tests and review. Phases 1–3 are each shippable alone; the pilot starts when Phase 2 lands, not on a calendar date. Roughly 240–325 h to the end of Phase 3b, 16–22 weeks at 15 h/week; the pilot starts after Phase 2 at roughly 150–210 h (10–14 weeks). **Placement of the second set:** the parts the pilot needs on night one are in Phase 2 — *Dnevnik* with the *Završena smjena* summary (settlement lives there), *Razgovor* as text with the "Za naručiti" note (the pilot needs "šta naručiti" and a place to confirm things from day one, and habits form in the first weeks — running Viber for half the pilot means two places), and the *Raspored* grid with copy/publish and the waiter's read-only week (swapping is "common here", so the schedule must be in the app before the first swap is negotiated in it). Everything else from R1–R5 — photos and camera, forwarding and the Telegram chat digest, Dnevnik filters, the laptop table and the per-kind audit of the Telegram mirror (the mirror itself ships in Phase 2, F13 a), the swap lifecycle and *Sati* — is a new **Phase 3b — Ekipa** that runs during the pilot next to Phase 3, because none of it produces data the owner is paying for and all of it benefits from a week of real messages and a real week of shifts. The junior-dev critique's re-phasing is followed for that reason; the alternative (all of R1–R5 after Phase 3) was rejected because it delays the chat and the schedule past the moment staff form habits (§17).

## 17. Rejected critique

- **Telegram one-time approval codes (`approval_codes`) and a separate owner `approval_pin_hash`:** unnecessary once the owner decides only from his own device; a request is `pending` for the seconds it takes him to tap *Odobri* on his phone. Revisit if the owner is often present but phoneless.
- **`terminal_ref` on every card payment:** 4 extra taps per card sale; the per-shift `card_diff_fen` against the Z total entered by the owner, plus per-waiter card share, catch the same fraud.
- **`queued_for_ms` from `performance.now()` for clock skew:** resets on every reload; heartbeat-measured `clock_skew_s` is simpler and survives restarts.
- **Reading `price_history` at lock with `next_shift` scheduling:** scheduling is dropped in v1; a price edit applies immediately and the lock keeps copying `products.price_fen`. Less code, one truth.
- **`continues_shift_id` "nastavak" shifts:** the auto-open rule already creates a new shift with the same business date after an early close; the owner sees two shifts on one date.
- **`refund_owed` cash movements:** replaced by "no money moves unless the owner says so" — a void applied after the requester's settlement moves his expected cash through a derived term (F10), not a row; an explicit `refund` row exists when cash actually left the drawer.
- **`stock_movements.pre_count_id` and re-flagging late sales:** solved more simply by `occurred_at` on movements, a theoretical that reads by `occurred_at`, and one `late_sync` offset row per late sale that lands behind a confirmed count.
- **"On hand = theoretical(now)" as a second definition of stock:** rejected; `SUM(qty_delta)` stays the only definition and the `late_sync` row keeps it true, so every page sums the same column.
- **Storing ticked line ids in `payments.covers_json`:** the ticks are an amount helper; only order client ids are stored, because the coverage rule needs orders and a line-level split is Phase 5.
- **Photos as archival attachments (Z reports, sealed envelopes, guest receipts):** file uploads add storage, UI and backup weight; Phase 5. The delivery-note photo and chat photos (R4) are the exceptions and share one `uploads` table and one route; only delivery photos are backed up.
- **The 10 s *Poništi* after lock:** deleted (two undo paths for one mistake); the 300 s self-void covers it.
- **`waiter_can_open_shift` setting:** superseded by auto-open on first lock; a blocked first hour is worse than an unset float.
- **Moving a tab onto an occupied table ("Sto 5 · 2 računa" stacking):** refused with `409 TABLE_OCCUPIED` and an explanation in v1; two bills on one table wait for merge in Phase 5.
- **Guest-side "free drink" rule as a product rule:** an owner decision written into *Pravila* if he wants it, not a feature.
- **Fixed 12–18 g tobacco band and quarter-tin counts for shifts:** replaced by measured grams ± 15 % and weighing; quarter-tin estimates survive only in the monthly full count.
- **Twenty-four-month retention job in v1:** the notice is v1; the task is Phase 5 — nothing reaches 24 months before then. The same reasoning moves 12-month chat-text redaction to Phase 5 and drops the design's `BEFORE DELETE` retention exception on `chat_messages` (a hard delete of a row referenced by a younger `reply_to_id` would also fail under `foreign_keys=ON`); the FKs are `ON DELETE SET NULL` and the redaction transition exists from Phase 0 so the task is trivial later.
- **"Zaključi smjenu" as the waiter's end-of-shift label (R3 design):** *zaključi* already means "lock this round" on the most tapped button in the app; the same word for "end my night" is a guaranteed mis-tap or a mental model where the last lock locks the shift. The label is *Završi smjenu*; the mechanism (`409 SETTLED` after `settle`) is the lock-in Vedran asked for.
- **A second end-of-shift endpoint or table for the waiter's lock-in:** `POST /api/shifts/:id/settle` is the lock-in; the summary is a column on `waiter_settlements` and an entry in `log_entries`. Two end-of-shift paths would be two places to forget.
- **Keeping `audit_log` next to `log_entries`, and the roster writing generic `entity/action` rows:** one who-did-what ledger, one writer (`log()`), one reader (*Dnevnik*); every roster write is a typed kind with a template, or the template test fails.
- **A frozen cash diff in the *Završena smjena* title and in Telegram:** contradicts the derived-diff contract (§8) and turns the log into the accusation feed it must not be; the body holds declared and expected-at-declare, the chip is live, Telegram carries the tolerance word only.
- **A Telegram message for every per-waiter settlement (`waiter_finished` unconditionally in `TELEGRAM_KINDS`):** five messages at midnight colliding with the 6/hour cap; the per-waiter lines ride in `shift_closed`, and only a settlement outside tolerance goes out on its own (`cash_variance`, the original rule, F13 a).
- **`users.gender` for two verb templates:** noun-phrase titles ("Završena smjena · Amar") need no gender column.
- **Chat messages riding the money outbox:** one unsent "nema leda" would block a cash handover and a count through `outbox_len` / `pending_count`; chat has its own pending list and never counts.
