## (a) Fiscalization: legal status and how to position the app

**Three jurisdictions, three regimes.** FBiH, RS and Brčko District each have their own fiscal law [V] (https://dddinvoices.com/de/learn/fiscalization-and-real-time-reporting-in-bosnia-and-herzegovina). In all three, a café is legally obliged to issue a fiscal receipt for every sale, whatever local practice is.

**FBiH — today.** Regime under the 2009 Zakon o fiskalnim sistemima: hardware fiscal device (fiscal printer or ECR with fiscal memory) + GPRS terminal; device *drivers* are certified but POS *software is not* certified; a receipt for every transaction, a printed daily report (dnevni izvještaj) per device [V] (https://www.fiscal-requirements.com/countries/4). Best-selling device in FBiH/Brčko is the locally made Tring FP1 Plus; a Tring FP1 C costs ~652 KM + PDV = 762,84 KM and ships with a free basic POS program [V] (https://www.fiskalna.ba/shop/fiskalni-uredaji/fiskalni-printeri, https://www.tring.ba/Home/DetailsView/?shortProizvoda=166-Tring-FP1-Plus-Fiskalni-printer&podKatId=45).

**FBiH — new law.** Zakon o fiskalizaciji transakcija u FBiH adopted 20–23 Jan 2026, in force 12 Feb 2026, but *not yet applied*: application starts after bylaws, at the latest 18 months after entry into force (≈ Aug 2027) [V] (https://upfbih.ba/usvojen-zakon-o-fiskalizaciji-transakcija-u-fbih, https://forbes.n1info.ba/ekonomija/sta-se-mijenja-za-firme-i-koji-su-rokovi-za-nove-fiskalne-kase-svi-detalji-zakona-o-fiskalizaciji-u-fbih/, https://www.akta.ba/vijesti/bih/201309/softver-umjesto-fiskalnih-kasa-sta-je-eset-i-kako-ce-raditi-nova-fiskalizacija-u-fbih). Key points: the fiscal cash register is replaced by **ESET** (Elektronski sistem za evidentiranje transakcija) — certified software on a phone, tablet, PC, POS or cloud, real-time reporting to Porezna uprava FBiH, digital signature + QR on receipts, deferred fiscalization when offline, a free Tax Administration app for small/non-PDV payers [V] (akta.ba above; https://www.paragraf.ba/dnevne-vijesti/17022026/17022026-vijest2.html). Transition for existing fiscal devices: Forbes says 2 years (B2C) / 3 years (B2B); akta.ba says 3–4 years — sources disagree, read the law text (https://parlamentfbih.gov.ba/v2/userfiles/file/Usvojeni%20materijali_2026/Zakon_fisk_transak%20-%20BOS.pdf). **Hospitality is named explicitly:** an ESET may compose and sign *narudžbenice* (order slips) and build the fiscal račun from them [V] (paragraf.ba above). Fines for serious violations 8.000–30.000 KM for legal entities [V] (Forbes above).

**RS.** Zakon o fiskalizaciji (Serbian model): electronic fiscal device = **ESIR** (certified invoicing software) + **PFR** (local LPFR or virtual VPFR) + a *bezbjednosni element* (card for LPFR, file for VPFR) issued by Poreska uprava RS; QR verification code on every receipt [V] (https://granicedoboja.info/u-rs-pocela-testna-faza-novog-fiskalnog-sistema-ovo-su-detalji-njegove-primjene/). ESIR certification is run by the Tax Administration (2025 checklist exists) [V] (https://www.fiscal-requirements.com/countries/5). Rollout: first group (old fiscal-register users, incl. cafés) had to start by 17 Jun 2024; second group by 31 Mar 2025; devices subsidised from the RS budget, typical maintenance ≈ 33 KM/month [V] (https://www.mojafirma.ba/fiskalizacija-republika-srpska/, https://zanatskakomorars.com/poreska-uprava-objavila-javni-poziv-za-drugu-grupu-obveznika-pocinje-drugi-krug-fiskalizacije-u-srpskoj/). Integration docs of one vendor: https://doc.ofs.ba/.

**Brčko.** Zakon o fiskalnim sistemima BD: hardware fiscal device with fiscal memory; software must sync the article base with the device (čl. 37); receipt for every sale whether or not asked (čl. 73); daily report (čl. 84); fines 2.000–15.000 KM (čl. 100); no e-fiscalization announced [V] (https://advokat-prnjavorac.com/Zakon-o-fiskalnim-sistemima-Brcko-distrikt-BiH.html, https://df.bdcentral.net/Content/Read/o%20fiskalizaciji?lang=hr).

**Positioning rules for Šank (so nobody mistakes it for a fiscal device):**
1. Name the artefact **narudžba**, never *račun*, *fiskalni račun*, *kasa*, *blagajna*. Any on-screen/printed summary carries the header `PREGLED NARUDŽBE — NIJE FISKALNI RAČUN`. This matches the "narudžbenica" concept the new FBiH law expects to precede the fiscal receipt.
2. Never show PDV lines, tax rates, fiscal counters (BF/IBFM/IBFU), a QR code, or a receipt-like numbering series. Order ids look like `#2026-09-08/042`.
3. Footer text in app and README: "Aplikacija služi internoj evidenciji narudžbi i stanja šanka. Nije fiskalni uređaj i ne zamjenjuje fiskalni račun."
4. Schema future-proofing only: `orders.fiscal_status` (`none|pending|fiscalized`, default `none`), `orders.fiscal_ref` (nullable text). Nothing else now.

**Later integration path [A]:** (FBiH today) drive a Tring/other fiscal printer through the vendor's certified Windows driver on the bar PC — POS software need not be certified today; (FBiH after ESET) either call a third-party certified ESET's API or get certified yourself (heavy; avoid); (RS) POST to a certified ESIR/LPFR local HTTP API, which in the Serbian model is an open spec — RS parity is an assumption. Design the order model as the input to any of these, never as the receipt itself.

## (b) How BiH cafés actually run

- **Smjene.** Two shifts are typical: *prva/jutarnja* (≈07–15) and *druga/večernja* (≈15–close); lounges often run one long evening shift [A]. FBiH hospitality law frames 06:00–24:00 in settlements; municipalities set exact hours and grant extensions (Općina Centar allowed 05:00 for a World Cup match; KS allowed 02:00 during SFF 2025) [V] (https://tvsa.ba/reagiranje-vlade-kantona-sarajevo-povodom-navoda-o-radnom-vremenu-ugostiteljskih-objekata/, https://www.oslobodjenje.ba/vijesti/sarajevo/opcina-centar-objekti-mogu-raditi-do-05-00-sati-u-noci-utakmice-bi-h-protiv-sad-a/, https://raport.ba/vlada-kantona-sarajevo-produzeno-radno-vrijeme-ugostiteljskim-objektima-tokom-sff-a/).
- **Pazar.** Cash is counted at shift close; the waiter/bartender hands cash to the owner (*predaja pazara*) and writes the total in a *sveska* (notebook) or on a paper *blok*; a shortage (*manjak*) is usually taken from the waiter, a surplus (*višak*) kept [A]. Without receipts, "pazar" is the only number the owner sees today — the app's shift close should produce `expected_cash` (sum of cash orders) vs `counted_cash` and a signed `difference`.
- **Stanje šanka.** Bartender counts bottles/cans at open and close ("početno/završno stanje"); consumption = početno + prijem − završno; owner compares with pazar. Formal *inventura* monthly in cafés that keep books; weekly bar counts in tighter places [A]. FBiH has a legal notion of *kalo, rastur, kvar i lom* in accounting rules but I could not verify a percentage table for drinks; Croatia's HGK decision (2023/24) exists as a reference model [V] (https://www.teb.hr/novosti/2023/nova-odluka-o-dopustenom-kalu-rastepu-kvaru-i-lomu-u-ugostiteljstvu/). Model waste as explicit `stock_movements.type = 'waste'` with a reason, not a percentage.
- **Deliveries.** Coca-Cola HBC B-H (Hadžići plant; DCs in Tuzla, Ljubuški, Laktaši, Bihać) serves 14,000+ outlets directly [V] (https://ba.coca-colahellenic.com/ba/about-us/ukratko-o-coca-coli-hbc-b-h-sarajevo); HoReCa gets **returnable 0,25 l glass** [V] (https://www.coca-cola.com/content/dam/onexp/ba/bs/sac-loyalty/bih_horeca_pravila.pdf), sold by the *gajba* of 24 [V, regional listings] (https://www.t-rex.rs/sokovi/sokovi-gazirani/sokovi-gazirani-u-flasici/coca-cola-0-25-24-komada-u-gajbi/). Weekly sales-rep visit + delivery with *otpremnica/faktura*, empties (*ambalaža*) exchanged [A]. Beer (Sarajevska/Banjalučka/Tuzlanska), coffee (Vispak/Zlatna džezva, Grand, Franck, local roasters), water (Sarajevski kiseljak, Olimpija), spirits and snacks come via 2–4 regional *veleprodaja* distributors or cash-and-carry (Metro/Bingo) [A]. Prijem robe screen: `supplier`, `document_no`, `date`, lines `stock_item_id, qty, pack_size, unit_cost`.
- **Typical lounge menu and 2026 price bands (Sarajevo; provincial towns ~20–30 % lower [A]):** espresso 2–3 KM [A]; kafa/cappuccino avg 4,01 KM (3–5) [V]; Coca-Cola 0,25/0,33 avg 3,84 KM (3–4) [V]; water 0,33 2–3 KM [V]; domestic draft 0,5 l 4–6 KM [V]; imported beer 0,33 ~5 KM [V] (https://www.numbeo.com/cost-of-living/in/Sarajevo, updated 28 Aug 2026); coffee rising toward 4 KM everywhere [V] (https://biznis.ba/vijesti/bih/poskupljuje-kafa-cijene-omiljenog-napitka-dosezu-i-do-4-km/94780). Čaj 2,5–4 KM, cijeđeni sok 5–7 KM, energetsko piće 0,25 5–6 KM, kokteli 8–15 KM, žestoka 0,03 l 3–6 KM, grickalice 3–6 KM [E]. Restaurant/hotel price index +4,8 % YoY (Jul 2025→Jul 2026) [V] (https://www.vrisak.info/gospodarstvo/od-kave-do-sisanja-pogledajte-koliko-su-u-bih-poskupjeli-svakodnevni-proizvodi-i-usluge-u-pet-godina/).

## (c) Shisha specifics

- **Brands seen in Sarajevo lounges:** Al Fakher, Adalya, Starbuzz, Fumari, Darkside, Element [V] (https://www.damask.ba/, https://www.funfactory.hr/blogs/news/nargila-barovi-u-sarajevu); regional shops also carry Serbetli, Musthave, Tangiers, Al Waha, 187, Holster, Hookain [V] (https://nargilashopbeograd.rs/en/shop/aroma-za-nargila/al-fakher-flavour/). Retail in BiH (OLX): Al Fakher 250 g ≈ 25 KM, 1 kg ≈ 80 KM; Adalya 1 kg ≈ 75 KM [V] (https://olx.ba/artikal/6877705/al-fakher-duhan-za-nargilu/, https://olx.ba/artikal/37946562/okusi-za-nargilu-adalya-1kg-aroma-arome-okus-nargila/).
- **Pack sizes** [A, standard for the brands]: Al Fakher 50 g / 250 g / 1 kg; Adalya 50 / 200 / 250 g / 1 kg; Serbetli 50 / 250 / 500 g / 1 kg; Fumari 100 g; Darkside 30 / 100 / 250 g; Musthave 25 / 125 g. Store tobacco in **grams**, one `stock_item` per flavour, `pack_size_g` on the delivery line.
- **Per-bowl consumption (normativ)** [A]: light lines (Al Fakher, Adalya, Serbetli) 15–20 g; Darkside/Musthave (heavier) 20–25 g; mixes split grams across 2–3 flavours. Coal: coconut cubes (26 mm) — Tom Cococha, Cocobrico, Coco Nara, Black Coco; 1 kg ≈ 72 cubes [A]; ~€12–18/kg regionally [V] (https://kanabi.hr/coco-ugljen1kg) → 25–35 KM/kg landed [E]; a session burns 3–4 cubes to start plus 3–4 replacements (*zamjena žara*), ≈ 8 cubes ≈ 110 g. Model `products.kind = 'shisha'` with `recipe` rows: `{stock_item: flavour, qty_g: 20}` + `{stock_item: coal, qty_pcs: 8}`.
- **Pricing conventions** [A/E]: per bowl; standard 12–18 KM, premium/dark lines 18–25 KM, *zamjena lule* (fresh bowl, same session) 8–12 KM, extra coal free or 2 KM. Serbian reference: Sahara Beograd charges 700 RSD standard / 800 RSD premium & nicotine ≈ 11,7 / 13,4 KM [V] (https://www.sahara.rs/stranica/cenovnik). No BiH lounge publishes a cjenovnik online — ask the owner and treat these as seed data.
- **Counting practice** [A]: lounges count *lule* per shift (owner cross-checks bowls sold vs grams gone); weigh the open tin at close on a kitchen scale. Screen "Popis nargila": per flavour `expected_g` (from recipe × sold) vs `counted_g`.
- **Compliance facts the owner cares about:** shisha tobacco must carry UINO excise stamps; UINO seized 135 kg of unstamped shisha tobacco in retail checks [V] (https://n1info.ba/vijesti/oduzet-duhan-za-nargilu-u-vrijednosti-od-32-800-km/, https://www.uino.gov.ba/portal/bs/akciza-i-putarina/obiljezavanje-i-prometovanje-akcize?print=print) — so `deliveries.supplier` + `document_no` doubles as proof of origin. FBiH tobacco-control law bans smoking indoors; the "posebna prostorija za pušenje" rulebook (Sl. novine FBiH 41/24, in force 13 Jun 2024, 6+6-month grace) is the exception path; treatment of nargila bars was contested in amendments [V] (https://fmoh.gov.ba/zakoni/18/zakon-o-kontroli-i-ogranicenoj-upotrebi-duhana-duhanskih-i-ostalih-proizvoda-za-pusenje, https://www.072info.com/amandmanima-na-zakon-o-zabrani-pusenja-se-favoriziraju-nargila-barovi-a-potapaju-ugostitelji-i-privreda/). Not an app concern beyond not making claims.

Vocabulary notes: use *sedmica* (not *tjedan*), *kafa* (not *kava*), month names *januar … decembar*, weekdays *pon uto sri čet pet sub ned*; *lokal* for the venue; avoid *račun* except in "Na račun kuće".

**Formatting** [A — standard practice; verify `Intl` output once in Node]: money `1.250,50 KM` (dot thousands, comma decimals, space, "KM"; never "BAM" in UI); `new Intl.NumberFormat('bs-BA',{style:'currency',currency:'BAM'})` should yield this, else format the number and append ` KM`. Quantities `2×`, volumes `0,25 l`, `0,33 l`, `0,5 l`, weights `20 g`. Dates `08.09.2026.` (trailing dot is correct Bosnian; drop it in tight UI), short `08.09.`, time 24 h `22:35`, datetime `08.09.2026. 22:35`. `Intl.DateTimeFormat('bs-BA')` gives `8. 9. 2026.` — use a custom `dd.MM.yyyy` formatter. Accept both `,` and `.` on decimal inputs (Android keyboards differ). Store UTC ISO strings; TZ `Europe/Sarajevo`.

- **International:** Loyverse free core (POS, KDS, CDS, 31-day analytics); add-ons 5 $/mo per store (history), 5 $/mo per employee, 25 $/mo per store (advanced inventory) [V] (https://loyverse.com/pricing); it does not fiscalize in BiH/RS [A]. **Square** only US/UK/IE/CA/AU/JP [V] (https://community.squareup.com/t5/General-Discussion/International-Availability-of-Square-Online/m-p/297849); **SumUp** lists Croatia, Slovenia, Bulgaria but not Serbia or BiH [V] (https://www.sumup.com/en-us/). Card terminals in BiH come from the venue's bank [A].
- **What Šank could charge [E]:** cafés already pay ~33 KM/mo for RS fiscal maintenance and 42–135 KM/mo (22–69 €) for regional POS suites. A non-fiscal order+stock+accountability tool for one venue: **29 KM/mo** (orders + shifts), **59 KM/mo** (+ stock, deliveries, counts), **99 KM/mo** (multi-venue/owner group), annual 10 months for 12. Anchor: below POS Sector Standard (≈76 KM) since it lacks fiscalization.

## (f) Practical realities

- **Devices:** Android 69,4 % / iOS 30,6 % of mobile in BiH (Aug 2026) [V] (https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/bosnia-and-herzegovina) → PWA installed from Chrome, large tap targets, works on 3–4-year-old mid-range Androids; test iOS Safari PWA too.
- **Payments:** card turnover 21,1 bn KM in 2025 but 68 % of card value is ATM withdrawals, 28 % POS; a third of cardholders still prefer cash [V] (https://www.bl-portal.com/ekonomija/rekordnih-21-milijardu-km-provuceno-kroz-kartice-u-bih-se-najvise-koriste-za-podizanje-gotovine/, https://www.oslobodjenje.ba/vijesti/bih/koliko-bh-gradani-placaju-karticom-od-ovog-podatka-ce-vam-se-zavrtjeti-u-glavi-1027789/). Default `payment_method = 'cash'`, one tap to `card`.
- **Messaging:** Viber dominates BiH/Serbia (90 %+ of smartphone users daily), Croatia prefers WhatsApp [V] (https://www.ladiesin.ba/whatsapp-ili-viber-koju-aplikaciju-koriste-razlicite-generacije/, https://mitto.ch/12-viber-statistika-koje-brendovi-ne-mogu-ignorisati/). Owner alerts: web-push first (already shipped in snajper), Telegram bot as a fallback; Viber bot API exists but is a paid/approval channel — skip [A].
- **Business day:** lounges legally close 24:00–02:00 with frequent extensions (see (b)); define `business_date = local_time − 4 h` (cutoff **04:00**) so a 01:30 order belongs to the previous day; shifts reference `business_date`, reports group by it.
- **Staff:** chronic shortage; Sarajevo employers offer 1.200–1.500 KM for waiters and still lose them to Croatia (2.800–4.500 KM) [V] (https://www.klix.ba/biznis/posao/ugostitelji-u-sarajevu-konobarima-nude-do-1-200-km-platu-ali-ih-sve-teze-pronalaze/220524049, https://www.fokus.ba/biznis/ugostitelji-u-bih-traze-konobare-nudimo-platu-i-do-1-500-km/2354558/, https://scena.ba/u-bih-place-konobara-od-1400-do-1500-km-a-u-hrvatskoj-mogu-zaraditi-i-do-4500-km/). Turnover of months, not years [A] → 4-digit PIN login, `users.active` toggle instead of delete, onboarding under 2 minutes, no per-seat pricing later.
- **Connectivity:** flaky Wi-Fi, mobile data common [A] → queue locked orders in IndexedDB and replay; show "Van mreže — narudžba će biti poslana" rather than blocking.
