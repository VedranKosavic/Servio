- Table naming: risk §4 uses orders + order_rounds + order_items; mvp §4 and user §4 use tabs + orders + order_lines. Ruling: tabs/orders/order_lines — 'narudžba' = one locked tura, which is what the waiter says out loud.

Rationale: As someone who has been robbed by staff and also carried a pouch for years, I want two things: the truth about cash and stock as early as possible, and rules my honest people can read on the wall. 'user' wins (50) because its product is the one a waiter will tolerate and an owner will actually use — the Fairness contract in §7, the S12 Pravila screen, the reply notes, 'Ovo potvrđuje šanker' instead of 'nemate pravo', a Pregled dana header that shows cash diff, manjak KM and g/bowl, and a Phase 4 exit that says the owner runs the 5-minute review alone. Its weaknesses are about ordering and hours, not design, and those are fixable by grafting: 'mvp' (45) gets the roadmap order right — shifts, blind cash, voids/comps and the spot count before modifiers, split bills and SSE — and has the settings (approver_roles, waiter_can_open_shift) that make the app survive a night with no šanker, but its hour estimates are fantasy, it deploys too late, allows one payment per tab and pushes every fairness tool to Phase 5. 'risk' (44) has the sharpest enforcement ideas — block the handover while a phone still holds unsent rounds, recompute every closed shift nightly and shout on drift, the Phase 0 ledger test list, the simulated night 'to the fen' — but it is months of work for a 2–4 person lounge, assumes a bartender and a shared bar device every night, and spends its length on Phase 5 analytics nobody needs before there are three months of trustworthy data. The final plan is therefore 'user' design and hours, 'mvp' phase order and small-lounge settings, and 'risk' enforcement rules and acceptance tests; rulings above resolve the cutoff (06:00), realtime (poll first), void/comp storage (line_adjustments + one-transition trigger; charged_fen on order_lines), multiple payments per tab, settlement block, and deploy in Phase 0.

It puts the complete accountability loop — waiter locks a round, blind cash settlement, blind spot count on ~15 high-value items, owner's 5-minute Pregled dana — in front of real staff before any analytics, while fixing the ledger shape (feninga, base units, venue_id on every table, server-snapshotted prices, append-only triggers, sale movements inside the lock transaction) from Phase 0 so that everything deferred is additive. Its dashboard is scoped to money truth with the attention queue at the centre, and its open questions each map to a setting or flow. Its real failings are estimate honesty (a 14-hour waiter app with an outbox and PWA is not achievable by a junior + Opus pair), deploying too late for real-phone offline tests, and a few missing mechanisms (payouts entry, post-lock comps, table handover, drift check). The risk draft has the strongest tests, integrity alarm and metric definitions, but its build-everything-then-pilot schedule (55–65 working days, roughly six months at 15 h/week) is the pattern that kills hospitality pilots: no real-night feedback until the design is frozen. The user draft is the closest runner-up, with the only realistic effort model, the best onboarding (the Postavljanje lokala wizard is also the strongest SaaS asset of the three) and the most complete flows, but Phase 1 is too fat to reach real nights on schedule and it pulls stock deduction out of the core transaction until Phase 3. The final plan is therefore the mvp skeleton, re-costed with user's per-phase hours (pilot around week 8–10, not week 4), deployed and backed up in Phase 0, with risk's test list, nightly recompute, metric thresholds and settlement block grafted in, and user's wizard, waiter onboarding, Pravila screen, payouts step and post-lock comp semantics added. Two gaps none of the drafts covers — a mechanism behind 'Predaj sto kolegi' and a whole-tab table move — are ruled into Phase 1 as tabs.assigned_to / tabs.table_id updates with audit rows.

## Critics

### thief

The ledger design is strong against the crude tricks (editing lines, changing prices, deleting rounds, silent voids, post-payment voids) and the "pending does not reduce expected" rule is the best single idea in the plan. What it does not close is the front door: the app only sees what I choose to tap, and its reconciliation net (~15 spot items, quarter-tin tobacco estimates, a ±30 % g/bowl band, per-shift rather than per-custodian variance) is too coarse to see unrecorded coffees, soft drinks and packed-light bowls. The second structural hole is trust in things the staff type or hold: the owner's PIN on my phone, a self-reported outbox count, a typed card Z total, unapproved payouts and an editable opening float. The fixes are cheap and mostly rules and columns rather than features: a bartender prep list ("no ticket, no drink"), weigh-based counts with a scale before the pilot, owner approval via the owner's own device or one-time code, server-side staleness checks on settlement and counts, pending status on payouts, float continuity with an `owner_pickup` movement, and per-approver / void-then-pay / card-share reports. With those, the remaining exploits are the ones every bar has and no software fixes — whole-staff collusion and pour size — which the plan already assigns to owner process.

- **[critical] Not entering the round at all is still free for everything outside the ~15 spot items (kafa, čaj, sokovi, voda)** — §7 risk 1 admits this is the main theft path but the only mechanisms are the spot count of ~15 `is_spot` items, a weekly g/bowl proxy and a monthly mystery guest. In a Bosnian lounge the highest-volume cash item is coffee: 7 g from a 1 kg bag at ~2 KM a cup. Nothing in the plan counts coffee, tea, sugar or cheap soft drinks per shift; the monthly *Inventura* on a 1 kg bag with a tin-granularity count cannot see 30 unrecorded coffees a night (210 g ≈ 60 KM/night). The 'Sync gap' metric (≥3 open tables, no lock for 45 min) is defeated by locking one cheap item every 30 min. There is no mechanism that ties *serving* a drink to a locked round: on nights with a šanker he still makes whatever the waiter shouts, and F2/F3 only start when I choose to tap.
  - Fix: (1) Add a bartender screen S13 *Priprema* (`/k/priprema`, polls `/api/tables/state` + new `GET /api/orders/pending-prep` every 10 s): locked `order_lines` not yet marked `prepared` (an `order_events(type='prepared')` row, one tap). House rule in *Pravila*: 'Šank pravi samo ono što je zaključeno' — no ticket, no drink. (2) Put coffee and sugar on the `is_spot` list with `count_method='weigh'` and `tare_g` (kitchen scale is a Phase 3 done-criterion, not 'once the owner buys one'); add a per-shift derived metric `g kafe / prodana kafa` with the same band logic as g/bowl. (3) Guest-side rule on the table tent and in *Pravila*: 'Ako vam konobar ne pokaže pregled narudžbe na telefonu, piće je besplatno' — this makes the guest the enforcer at zero cost. (4) Compute `promet/h` per waiter from shift 1 (owner-only, no flagging until 10 shifts) so the owner can compare it with what he sees on the floor.
- **[critical] The owner's PIN typed on my phone is harvested once and then approves everything, including `owner_guest`** — §2: 'the owner's PIN works on any waiter's phone for on-the-spot approval'; F6/F7 put the PIN pad on the waiter's own device. A 4-digit PIN entered on a phone I control is trivially captured (screen recording, shoulder view, Chrome remote debugging over USB reading the numpad DOM). After that I self-approve voids before payment, `complaint`/`promo` comps and `owner_guest` comps (which only the owner's PIN can vouch for) at will; every row shows `approved_by = owner`, so the reports treat them as legitimate and they are excluded from Na račun kuće % ('excluding owner_guest'). The lockout protects against guessing, not against capture.
  - Fix: Never accept the owner's PIN on a device with `mode='personal'` bound to someone else or on a shared staff device. Owner approval happens on the owner's own session: (a) the request goes `pending` and appears in *Zahtijeva pažnju* on his phone (one-tap *Odobri* already exists), or (b) for on-the-spot cases the waiter's sheet shows 'Traži kod od vlasnika' and the Telegram bot sends the owner a 6-digit `approval_codes(id, venue_id, adjustment_id, code, expires_at 120 s, used_at)`; the code is single-use and bound to that `line_adjustments.id`. Keep bartender PIN-on-phone approval (his exposure is bounded by his own stats). If the owner insists on PIN approval anyway, make it a separate 6-digit `approval_pin_hash`, and every use on a non-owner device sends an immediate Telegram 'Tvoj PIN je upotrijebljen na Amarovom telefonu, sto 7, storno 12 KM' so a stolen PIN is discovered the first time it is used.
- **[critical] Shisha off-book bowls hide inside recipe slack, quarter-tin counts and a ±30 % band** — §8: recipe 20 g/bowl (owner-editable, unverified), open tins counted 'in quarters' (a 250 g tin → 62,5 g granularity, i.e. ±3 bowls per tin), tolerance 15 g, g/bowl band ±30 % (14–26 g) evaluated per week. Real packing for a phunnel is often 12–15 g (assumption, brand-dependent). If the recipe says 20 g and I pack 13 g, every three recorded bowls leave one bowl of tobacco 'in the recipe' that I sell for 15 KM cash with no entry — my recorded g/bowl stays inside the band and the quarter-tin count cannot see 13 g. Coal: recipe 3 + 2 per top-up; I skip logging *Žar* (0 KM, nobody cares) or use 2 coals, so coal/bowl looks fine while bowls are missing. `is_spot` coal tolerance 4 kom ≈ one bowl's coal per shift for free.
  - Fix: (1) Measure, don't assume: at onboarding the owner packs 10 bowls per product on a scale; `shisha_grams` is set from that mean and stored with `measured_at`; the band becomes ±15 % (`gpb_band_pct:15`). (2) Weigh every open tin at every spot count (`count_method='weigh'`, `tare_g` per brand) — make the scale a Phase 3 done-criterion; the 'quarters' estimate is allowed only for `kind='full'` monthly counts, never for the shift spot count. (3) Evaluate g/bowl and coal/bowl per shift (already have `shift_summaries.bowls, tobacco_g, coals`), not per week, and show both on the shift header. (4) Count *lule* another way: a physical proxy the waiter cannot skip — a coal tolerance of 2 kom, and the bartender's *Priprema* rule (issue 1) applies to nargila too: no lit coal leaves the bar for an unlocked bowl. (5) Add `bowls_per_coal_box` to the weekly *Sumnjivo* checks: coal boxes consumed × pieces/box ÷ recorded bowls > 5 → flag.
- **[major] Void-then-pay with a colluding bartender: `was_paid=0`, under the 3 % storno threshold, stock re-added** — F6: a bartender in `approver_roles` (default) approves any void with his PIN on my phone, no time limit, no queue, no Telegram; `void_after_payment` fires only for `was_paid=1`. So: guest pays 47,50 in cash; I do not record the payment; I void a 12 KM whisky and a 15 KM nargila as `not_served` with the bartender's PIN; then I record `pay` 20,50. The storno % metric is per shift: 3 % of a 1.500 KM night is 45 KM per night, forever inside the yellow band, and the void writes `sale_storno` so the shelf matches too. Nothing reports voids per *approver*, nor the 'storno pa naplata za < 5 min' sequence that every experienced owner recognises.
  - Fix: (1) Add the pattern report `storno_then_pay`: an applied void on a tab followed by a `payments` row within 5 min → listed in *Zahtijeva pažnju* with both rows, counted per waiter per 30 days. (2) Per-approver strip on the Smjena page: `odobrio` count and KM per shift, per (approver, requester) pair per 30 days; > 3 approvals for the same requester in a shift → flag. (3) Bartender approval on the spot is allowed only when `seconds_since_lock ≤ 900`; older lines always go `pending` to the owner. (4) Threshold `void_bartender_shift_fen:3000`: bartender-approved voids beyond 30 KM in one shift send Telegram `void_approver_cap`. (5) `not_served` voids do not require the drink to be poured back: keep `sale_storno` only for `wrong_entry`, `guest_changed_mind`, `not_served` when `seconds_since_lock ≤ 900`.
- **[major] `walked_out` and `complaint` voids re-add stock that was consumed, so the shelf 'balances' after a walk-out I invented** — F6 reason chips include `walked_out` and `complaint`; §8: 'Applied voids write sale_storno'. A walked-out guest drank the drinks, so the storno puts 2 Red Bull and 30 ml whisky back on paper that are not on the shelf. That is a stock surplus of exactly what I sold off-book to the next table — the two errors cancel and the spot count is green. The plan already has the correct instrument for walk-outs (`tabs.status='unpaid'`, `pending_review`, stays in my expected cash) but offers the void path in parallel, and comps correctly do not reverse stock (F7 decision) while voids for the same physical situation do.
  - Fix: Remove `walked_out` from void reasons; the only path is `⋯ → Nije plaćeno` (tab-level, pending_review, in my expected cash until the owner decides). Add `reverses_stock` per reason in `shared/constants.ts`: `wrong_entry`, `guest_changed_mind`, `not_served` → `sale_storno`; `complaint`, `other` → no stock reversal (the drink existed). Show 'Vraća robu na stanje: da/ne' on the void sheet so the rule is visible. Test in Phase 2: a `complaint` void leaves `stock_movements` unchanged.
- **[major] Cash recorded as *Kartica*: `card_total_fen` is typed by the closer and never checked against Σ card payments** — F5 lets any waiter choose `Gotovina`/`Kartica`; F10 has the closer type `card_total_fen` from the Z report; §4 `shift_summaries` has `card_fen` but no `card_diff_fen`, and no alert or attention item compares the two. If I mark a 40 KM cash bill as card, my expected cash drops 40 KM and the terminal is 40 KM short — which nobody sees. If the closer is me (bartender or the waiter allowed to close) I type the Z to match. If there is no terminal at all (open question 7) the *Kartica* button is a pure hole. Card share per waiter is not reported either.
  - Fix: (1) `venues.settings.payment_methods: ['cash']` by default; *Kartica* only appears when the owner enables it. (2) `shift_summaries.card_diff_fen = card_total_fen − Σ card payments` (reversals included); any non-zero value lands in *Zahtijeva pažnju* and in the `shift_closed` Telegram line; `|card_diff| > 0` requires a note like the cash tolerance. (3) `card_total_fen` is entered by the owner in F11 (from the terminal or the bank statement), not by the closer; the closer only photographs the Z (`shifts.z_photo_path`). (4) Per-waiter `card_share_pct` on the Smjena strip and in the 30-day deviation table (> 2× own median → flag). (5) `payments.method='card'` requires `terminal_ref` (last 4 digits of the slip number) — 4 taps, and it makes fabricated card payments auditable against the terminal journal.
- **[major] `pending_count` is self-reported by my phone and goes stale offline; settlement, count and shift close all trust it** — §6/F10: the settlement block keys on `devices.pending_count` from the last heartbeat. In airplane mode no heartbeat arrives, so the server keeps the last value (0). I take orders offline all night, then before *Predaj gotovinu* I clear Chrome site data (Settings → Site settings → Clear & reset) — the outbox and cookie are gone, the plan itself says 'treat the outbox as volatile'. I re-enrol tomorrow blaming the phone. Rounds I chose to lock while online are the only ones that exist. The 'device with unsent rounds' attention item is fed by the same self-report. The plan says 'the reconciliation, not the phone, is the accountability mechanism' — but the reconciliation only covers 15 items (issue 1).
  - Fix: (1) Server-side staleness: `POST /api/shifts/:id/settle` must originate from the waiter's own `device_id` and the client flushes the outbox in the same call chain; the server refuses (`409 STALE_DEVICE`) when `devices.last_seen_at` is older than 120 s or the request body's `outbox_len ≠ 0`; override = approver PIN + `audit_log`, and the override count per waiter per 30 days is a deviation metric. (2) Log every heartbeat gap > 10 min during a shift as `order_events(type='device_offline', payload {from,to})` and show it on the waiter's row ('offline 21:10–23:40'); per-waiter offline minutes per 30 days is a published metric. (3) Rule in *Pravila*: rounds that never arrive count as unrecorded sales; a waiter who reports a 'lost phone' twice in 30 days works only on the shared bar device. (4) Require `navigator.storage.persist()` to be granted before the first lock and show 'Trajno spremanje: uključeno' in S11.
- **[major] *Isplate iz kase* (payouts) reduce expected cash with nothing but a note** — F10 step 4: amount + note → `cash_movements(type='payout')`, by the closer (bartender by default), no approval, no attachment, and it lowers `cash_expected` immediately. 'Dobavljač leda 60 KM', 'Taksi za robu 25 KM' every few nights is invisible inside a 5-minute review, and the plan's own rule for voids ('a pending void does not reduce expected cash') is not applied to payouts.
  - Fix: Treat payouts like voids: `cash_movements.status pending|approved|rejected` (one-transition trigger as on `line_adjustments`); a pending payout does not reduce `cash_expected`, so the closer physically hands over the cash short and the diff shows until the owner approves. Add `reason` enum `dobavljac|sitno|ostalo`, `photo_path` (receipt), optional `delivery_id` link; the owner approves from *Zahtijeva pažnju*. Any payout > `payout_pin_fen:5000` needs the owner (issue 2 flow) at the time.
- **[major] Opening float is whatever the opener types; the drawer has no continuity from one shift to the next** — F1 step 2: `opening_float_fen` is 'prefilled from the last shift' but editable, and the owner 'can override' — so can the bartender who opens. Last night the closer counted 380 KM; tonight I open with 330 and pocket 50, or leave the drawer as is and declare 330: `cash_expected` starts 50 lower. There is no `cash_movements` type for the owner taking the pazar out of the drawer (`float_in|payout` only), so the plan cannot express 'counted 1.480 at close → owner took 1.300 → float 180', and nobody can tell whether the 50 KM vanished overnight or with the opener.
  - Fix: Add `cash_movements.type='owner_pickup'` (owner-only, amount, at close or in F11). Rule: `opening_float_fen` is computed = previous shift's `cash_counted_fen − Σ owner_pickup` and shown read-only; changing it requires the owner (issue 2 flow) and writes `audit_log` + a `float_override` line in *Zahtijeva pažnju*. Per-shift summary shows 'Ostalo u kasi sinoć / Početni float večeras / razlika'.
- **[major] Shift boundaries: close early and the last hour is off-book; sell after your own settlement; no shift open means the app is 'down'** — F10: `POST /api/orders` needs an open shift; after `status='closed'` nothing can be locked, so the closer (me, as bartender or the one waiter allowed) closes at 01:30 while guests stay until 03:00 — the best hour of a lounge is cash in my pocket, and the app itself looks compliant. Conversely with `waiter_can_open_shift=false` and no šanker, an unreachable owner means 'Smjena nije otvorena' — I take cash all night with a perfect excuse. Also, F10 settles per waiter *before* the closer's PIN: after my settlement (my `expected` revealed and handed over) I can still lock rounds and record cash payments with `paid_by=me` in the same shift; nothing refuses them, and they belong to no settlement.
  - Fix: (1) Server rule: after a `waiter_settlements` row exists for (shift, user), `POST /api/orders` and `/pay` with that user return `409 SETTLED`; S9 ends the session ('Odjavi se' is forced). A second settlement is allowed only via a new `waiter_settlements` row with `supersedes_id`. (2) When no shift is open, a PIN login by anyone auto-opens one with `shifts.auto_opened=1`, `opening_float_fen=NULL` (`float_missing` in *Zahtijeva pažnju*), instead of blocking sales — the app must never be the reason for off-book. (3) `venues.settings.closing_time` ('03:00'); a shift closed more than 60 min before it is listed as `early_close` with the last lock time; the `shift_closed` Telegram line shows 'zatvoreno 01:32 · zadnja narudžba 01:10'. (4) Owner may reopen a closed shift into a new 'nastavak' shift (`continues_shift_id`) so late guests are recorded, not hidden.
- **[major] Stock variance is attributed to 'the shift', so a missing bottle belongs to nobody** — §8: 'Variance is reported per shift (the people on it)'. With 3 waiters plus a bartender, a −1 Jack Daniel's is a quarter-suspicion for each and a full accusation for none — the exact 'gut feeling' problem the plan says it is solving for cash (`cash_custody='per_waiter'`). Since only one person stands behind the bar, the bartender knows every bottle he takes is diluted across the team; the plan has `witnessed_by` but no custodian.
  - Fix: Add `shifts.stock_custodian_id` (the šanker; on nights without one, the closer) and require a spot count at shift *open* by the custodian (`stock_counts.kind='spot'`, `phase open|close`) so the shift is bracketed: variance = close count − open count − sales + waste. Report `variance_fen` per custodian over 30 days in the same deviation table as cash diff, with the same 'označeno za razgovor' wording. Pilot rule in *Pravila*: waiters do not take bottles from the shelf; the custodian pours. The open count is the same ~15 items and under 5 min, so it fits F1 step 3 which already exists as an optional *Početno stanje*.
- **[major] Rounds left unsent through the spot count are deducted twice after `count_adjust`, producing surpluses that mask real theft** — F9: `theoretical_qty` is snapshotted at submit; the owner's *Primijeni* writes `count_adjust` to the counted value. If my rounds are still in the outbox at count time, theoretical is too high → red manjak (explained away as 'my phone'), the owner applies the adjustment, my rounds then sync with the old `shift_id` and write `sale` movements again → the next count shows a višak of exactly those items. Alternate the two nights and my real skims sit inside the noise; the 30-day cumulative is also polluted. Nothing blocks a count while devices have pending rounds, and nothing reconciles late `sale` rows against a count that already absorbed them.
  - Fix: (1) `POST /api/stock/counts` submit and `/confirm` both refuse (`409 PENDING_OUTBOX`) while any `shift_members` device reports `pending_count > 0` or a stale heartbeat (same rule as settlement, issue 7); override only by the owner and logged. (2) Any `sale`/`sale_storno` movement whose `client_created_at` precedes `stock_counts.submitted_at` of a confirmed count for that item and location gets `stock_movements.pre_count_id` set and is excluded from the next theoretical; it is listed under *Kasno sinhronizovano* with its KM effect. (3) Confirm recomputes variance at confirm time and shows the delta against the submitted snapshot.
- **[minor] `staff_drink` comps are capped by count, not by value or product** — F7: two self-authorised `staff_drink` comps per shift with no `max_unit_price` and no product restriction. I comp a 12 KM cocktail and a 15 KM nargila 'for myself' and sell them for cash: 27 KM/night, 3 waiters → 80 KM/night, always inside the rule and excluded from any approval. Na račun kuće % > 2 % is per waiter per shift and evaluated against gross, so on a 500 KM personal night 10 KM is fine anyway.
  - Fix: `venues.settings.staff_drink_max_fen:300` and/or `products.staff_drink_allowed=1` (kafa, čaj, voda, sok); anything else with reason `staff_drink` needs an approver. Show 'Osoblje: 1/2 (do 3 KM)' on the sheet. Report staff-drink KM per waiter per 30 days next to comp %.
- **[minor] `kind='free_text'` / *Ostalo* lines: pricing is unspecified and they deduct no stock** — §4 has `products.kind free_text` and `order_lines.free_text`, §10 says 'no price input', but a free-text product must get `charged_fen` from somewhere. If the waiter types the amount, I sell a 12 KM whisky as 'Ostalo 3 KM'. Even with a fixed price, an *Ostalo* line writes no `stock_movements` and is invisible in *Bez normativa* (which lists products, not lines), so a bottle sold as 'Ostalo' shows only as shelf manjak with no trail. Nothing reports free-text usage per waiter.
  - Fix: Free-text products carry a fixed `price_fen` like any product; seed tiers 'Ostalo 2 KM / 5 KM / 10 KM' and never let the client send an amount (Zod rejects `charged_fen` in `/api/orders` bodies — already implied, make it a Phase 0 test). Add `free_text_count`/`free_text_fen` per waiter per shift to the Smjena strip; > 3 lines or > 20 KM per shift → *Zahtijeva pažnju* with the typed names, and a one-tap 'Napravi proizvod' so recurring names become real products with recipes.
- **[minor] Waste under 10 KM needs no PIN and has no per-shift cumulative cap or per-person report** — §7 rule 9: PIN only above `waste_pin_threshold_fen` per event; 'waste over threshold' in *Zahtijeva pažnju* is per event. Two 'prosuto Red Bull' (4 KM each) and one 'razbijeno pivo' a night cover exactly the units I sold off-book, each below 10 KM, and waste per waiter appears nowhere in the deviation table.
  - Fix: Apply the threshold cumulatively: `waste_shift_fen` per user per shift (default 1000 fen); once exceeded every further waste needs an approver. Add `otpis %` (waste KM / own gross) per waiter to the 30-day deviation table with a 1 % band, and require the physical evidence rule in *Pravila*: broken glass and spilled bottles stay in the crate behind the bar until the custodian confirms (`waste_events.approved_by`) — an unconfirmed waste is listed like an unwitnessed count.
- **[minor] The 'blind' cash handover is not blind: S11 *Moja smjena* shows my live promet all night** — §7 rule 7 and F10 reveal `expected_fen` only after `declared_fen`, but S11 gives every waiter 'my promet, gratis, storna' during the open shift, which is Σ own cash payments to the fen. I declare exactly that number every time; the blind step only catches counting mistakes. The plan sells blind handover as an accountability mechanism it does not deliver.
  - Fix: During an open shift S11 shows counts and statuses (tabs, rounds, storna with decisions, comps used 1/2) but no KM totals; `GET /api/me/shift` returns money fields only after a `waiter_settlements` row exists for that shift (the *Sažetak* at close already does this). Document in *Pravila* why: 'Pazar vidiš odmah nakon predaje, da bi predaja bila bez gledanja'. Keep 30-day history fully visible.
- **[minor] *Predaj sto kolegi* needs no acceptance, so an unpaid tab can be parked on a colleague before the walk-out** — §4: handover updates `tabs.assigned_to` and writes an event, no receiver PIN. F10 says per-waiter expected cash includes 'own unpaid tabs still pending_review' — 'own' is undefined (opened_by? assigned_to? who tapped Nije plaćeno?). If it is `assigned_to`, I hand a 60 KM tab to Amar and mark it unpaid from my phone; the plan's stated aim 'nobody pays another's manjak' is violated by one tap.
  - Fix: `POST /api/tabs/:id/assign` requires the receiving waiter's PIN on the receiving device (or the receiver taps *Prihvati* on his S1 where the table shows 'Nudi ti: Sto 7'); until accepted the tab stays mine. Define `tabs.unpaid_by` (who tapped Nije plaćeno) and attribute pending unpaid tabs to `unpaid_by`; a tab can be marked unpaid only by its current `assigned_to`.
- **[minor] Next-day approval of a void on a cash-paid line turns a fake 'refund' into cash the owner pays me** — F6: a `was_paid=1` void goes pending, stays in my expected cash, I hand the money over; the owner approves in F11 and the reversing `payments` row lowers the shift's expected cash after it was counted — the shift now shows a surplus equal to the 'refund' and nothing says what happens to that money. In practice the owner hands it back to the waiter who 'refunded the guest from his pocket'. Invented complaints become a refund business, and the ledger looks tidy.
  - Fix: Rule in *Pravila* and code: refunds to guests are made only on the spot by an approver (issue 2/4 flows); a `was_paid=1` void decided after the shift closed writes the reversal plus `cash_movements(type='refund_owed', user_id, status pending)` that the owner must physically settle and mark, so the money movement is a separate, visible row; count `refund_owed` per waiter per 30 days as a deviation metric.

### waiter

The ordering core holds up on a bad Friday: 5-tap coffees, server-owned tabs, client UUIDs and a never-blocking outbox are the right shape, and the tap budgets for adding lines are honest. What breaks is everything around the lock: the shift gate refuses the first hour's orders when nobody has opened a shift, a dead phone's rounds are discarded after close, a waiter cannot borrow a colleague's phone, every PIN needs the server so offline approval and shared-device unlock are impossible, žar top-ups cost 5 taps for 0 KM so the coal proxy will never be entered, and paying — the action after every table — is buried behind a ⋯ menu and a method step with no help for six guests paying separately. Fairness also leaks: pending voids and late-sync tabs land in an immutable per-waiter diff that the morning approval cannot clear. None of this needs new infrastructure; auto-open shift on first lock, late attach to closed shifts, a borrowed-session flag, cached local PIN hashes, a 2-tap Žar carve-out, a direct Naplati bar with line-tick amounts, and live-derived settlement diffs fix the ranked issues inside the existing tables and the one-process design.

- **[critical] No open shift = no orders for the first hour (F1/F2, §7 rule 3)** — Guests arrive at 18:00; the šanker opens the shift at 19:52 (the plan's own example). With `waiter_can_open_shift` off, the waiter sees 'Smjena nije otvorena' and `POST /api/orders` has no `shift_id` to assign, so either the lock is refused or it lands in the outbox as a 4xx `failed` red card. Either way the first hour of drinks is served, cash is collected, and nothing is recorded — exactly the leakage the app exists to stop, and the honest waiter later shows an unexplained 'višak'. The extra 'Uđi u smjenu' tap on every login is a second small tax for the same reason.

### junior-dev

The plan is unusually well thought out for its size: the ledger shape (append-only tables, server-snapshotted prices, stock movements inside the lock transaction, client UUIDs with UNIQUE(venue_id, client_id)) is correct and worth keeping exactly as written, and the choice of one Node process + SQLite + polling is the right call for this developer. Its weaknesses are concentrated in three places: (1) the offline design is described at the level of "outbox with client_id" but several payloads reference ids that only exist on the server after a flush, so a coder will build something that cannot work with Wi-Fi off; (2) the lifecycle after a shift closes (pending voids decided next morning, late-synced rounds, phones that never come back) is undefined while shift_summaries is append-only and the nightly recompute alarms on any drift, so the integrity alarm will fire on legitimate events; and (3) a handful of small holes contradict the anti-theft stance (opener rewrites opening stock without confirmation, unlimited sub-threshold waste, blind handover defeated by the waiter's own screen, 4-digit approver PINs typed on the waiter's phone). The 120–165 h estimate to the end of Phase 3 is roughly 1.5–2x optimistic for a junior + AI pair debugging PWAs on real Android and iOS phones; the fix is to cut Phase 1 scope (one undo mechanism, no table move/handover, no Bez stola) rather than to add hours. With the fixes below the plan is executable and the accountability guarantees survive intact.

- **[critical] Offline payloads reference server-assigned ids that do not exist on the phone yet** — §4 says ids are client-generated only for outbox entities and server-generated otherwise; `tabs` are server-owned ("the server owns tab creation") and `order_lines.id` is server-assigned. But F5's outbox payload is `{client_id, tab_id, ...}` posted to `POST /api/tabs/:id/pay`, and F6's void request needs an `order_line_id`. With Wi-Fi off, a waiter locks the first round of table 7 (queued), then takes cash (queued) and possibly voids a line (queued). At that moment there is no `tab_id` and no `order_line_id` on the phone. An AI coder will either block pay/void while the outbox is non-empty (violating invariant 5 'offline never blocks paying') or send null ids and get 4xx → red 'failed' cards on every offline night. The Phase 1 done-criterion (two rounds and a payment queued with Wi-Fi off, appear exactly once) cannot pass as specified.
  - Fix: Make every row born in the outbox carry a client UUID: (1) `POST /api/orders` payload `lines[]` includes `id` (client UUID) and the server inserts `order_lines.id` verbatim; (2) drop `POST /api/tabs/:id/pay` and `/unpaid`; use `POST /api/payments` with `{client_id, table_id | null, order_client_ids[], method, amount_fen, received_fen, tip_fen}` — the server resolves the tab as 'the tab that contains those orders' (all must belong to one tab, else 422 `SPLIT_TAB`), and the same for `POST /api/tabs/unpaid`; (3) `POST /api/adjustments` references `order_line_id` = the client-generated line id; (4) outbox flush stays strictly sequential oldest-first and stops (does not skip) on a 4xx so a later `pay` never runs before its `order` — the red card then names the blocked entries. Move/assign (`/move`, `/assign`) stay online-only with a disabled state 'Nema veze' since they are rare. Add to Phase 0 test list: 'order + payment + void enqueued offline for a brand-new table replay in order and produce 1 tab, 1 order, 1 payment, 1 adjustment'.
- **[critical] Opening-stock edits at shift open write `opening` movements without owner confirmation — a nightly theft channel** — F1 step 3: 'Optional Početno stanje of is_spot items, prefilled from the last confirmed count; edits are logged and write `opening` movements only for changed lines'. This lets whoever opens the shift (the šanker, or a waiter when `waiter_can_open_shift`) lower on-hand by one bottle of Jameson every evening as an 'opening' movement. The evening's spot count then matches theoretical exactly; there is never a variance, and 'sumnjivo savršeno' does not catch it because the count is not perfect by typing, it is perfect by construction. This contradicts §8 'Counts adjust only when the owner confirms' and rule 1 of §7.
  - Fix: Remove the movement-writing path from F1. Step 3 becomes: opener may run *Početno stanje* as a `stock_counts(kind='spot', shift_id, status='submitted', counted_by=opener)` — blind like F9, `theoretical_qty` snapshotted, no movements. The owner confirms (→ `count_adjust`) from *Zahtijeva pažnju* like any count. `stock_movements.type='opening'` is reserved for the one-time venue setup (`ref_type='venue_setup'`) and the append-only test asserts no `opening` row has a `shift_id`. Expected stock for the evening spot count is then 'last confirmed count + deliveries − sales − waste', which is exactly the §8 formula and makes the open-count vs close-count pair a per-shift variance attributable to the people on it.
- **[critical] Post-close events (late-synced rounds, voids decided next morning) are undefined and will trip the nightly 'drift' alarm; late_sync tabs violate the one-open-tab index** — Three facts collide: (a) `shift_summaries` is append-only and written at close; (b) the 05:30 recompute treats any difference from ledger rows as a code bug and pages the owner on Telegram; (c) legitimate rows land after close — a pending `line_adjustments` approved by the owner at 10:00 writes a reversing `payments` row (F6 step 4) and `sale_storno` movements into the closed shift; a round queued on a dead phone arrives the next afternoon (§6 'late sync', also possible with no open shift at all); F11 says the owner decides pending voids in *Pregled dana*, which is after close by design. So every normal morning review produces a 'drift' alarm, and the owner learns to ignore the integrity alarm. Separately, §6 says a late round 'opens a new tab for the table with late_sync=1'; `tabs` has a partial unique index 'one open tab per table' — if new guests already sit at table 5 with an open tab, the late insert fails with a constraint error → 4xx → the outbox marks the round `failed`; if it succeeds, F10 step 1 blocks the next shift close on a tab nobody can pay.
  - Fix: Define the after-close rules once, in §4 next to `shift_summaries`: (1) `payments`, `line_adjustments`, `stock_movements` get a `decided_in_shift_id`/`shift_id` = the shift OPEN at write time (nullable when none is open) plus the original `tab.shift_id` is reachable via the tab; the nightly recompute for shift S reads only rows with `shift_id = S AND created_at <= S.closed_at` and compares them to `shift_summaries` — anything else is by definition not drift. (2) Pregled dana shows a separate block 'Nakon zatvaranja: 1 storno odobren (−12,00 KM), 2 kasne ture (23,50 KM)' computed live from rows with `tab.shift_id = S AND created_at > S.closed_at`; cash reversals approved next day reduce the NEXT shift's expected cash for the owner/bartender (they hand the money back or keep it as an owner-noted discrepancy) — never the waiter's already-signed settlement. (3) A late round whose `client_created_at < the most recently closed tab on that table` (or that arrives with the tab's shift closed) creates `tabs(status='unpaid', pending_review=1, late_sync=1, unpaid_reason='late_sync')`, never `status='open'`, so the partial unique index and the close blocker are untouched; it appears in *Zahtijeva pažnju* with two buttons: 'Naplaćeno kroz [B]' (writes a cash payment `paid_by=B` into the current open shift, or `shift_id=NULL` if none) and 'Nije plaćeno' (approved unpaid). (4) A round arriving with no open shift and `client_created_at` inside a closed shift attaches to that shift with `late_sync=1` under rule 3; outside any shift → `422 NO_SHIFT` and the red card.
- **[major] 4-digit approver PIN typed on the waiter's own phone is the weakest link in the approval chain** — §2 and §7 rule 8: the owner's PIN 'works on any waiter's phone' and only the owner's PIN vouches `owner_guest` comps; the šanker's PIN approves voids and post-payment comps. A 4-digit PIN entered on the waiter's phone, in front of the waiter, on a busy floor, is shoulder-surfed in a week. After that the waiter approves his own post-payment voids (the exact fraud rule 6 targets) and `owner_guest` comps with no second person involved, and every such row reads `approved_by=owner`. Nothing in *Zahtijeva pažnju* distinguishes 'owner approved from /a' from 'owner PIN typed on Amar's phone'. Lockout does not help against a correct PIN.
  - Fix: (1) `users.pin_len` by role: 4 digits for waiters, 6 for owner/bartender (`pin_hash` unchanged; the numpad simply accepts 6). (2) Every decision records `decided_on_device_id`; when it differs from the approver's own bound device, the row gets `foreign_device=1`, is listed in *Zahtijeva pažnju* as 'Odobreno na Amarovom telefonu 22:41 · storno 12,00 KM', and an immediate Telegram line goes to the owner for `owner_guest` comps and any void with `was_paid=1` (rule `approval_on_foreign_device`, deduped per row). The owner sees a stolen PIN within minutes and can reset it from *Osoblje*. (3) Prefer remote approval: the owner decides from `/a` (Telegram deep link) which needs no PIN on anyone's phone; the sheet on the waiter's phone offers 'Pošalji vlasniku' first and the PIN pad second. (4) Approver PIN sheet lists approver names (the request carries `approver_user_id` + pin) so lockout stays keyed by device+user as designed.
- **[major] Timeline is 1.5–2x optimistic; cut Phase 1 scope and remove the redundant second undo mechanism** — Phase 1 at 35–50 h contains: six screens with a search/favourites/recent picker, the shisha builder, Pinia+idb-keyval cart and outbox with sequential flush/backoff/failed-card UX, PWA with service worker and update prompt, the lock transaction with tab find-or-create and recipes, multi-payment pay flow, tables-state polling, Premjesti/Predaj sto, Bez stola, the 10 s undo, minimal shift open, heartbeat, device self-enrolment, plus real-phone offline testing on Android and iOS and a Friday shadow run. The plan also carries two undo mechanisms for the same mistake: the 10 s *Poništi* at lock (F3 step 5, writes void adjustments for every line + storno movements) and the 120 s self-void (F6). Two code paths, two sets of tests, same guarantee.
  - Fix: Phase 1 scope becomes S0, S1, S2, S3, S4, S5, S8, `POST /api/orders`, `POST /api/payments` (cash + card, multiple), `/api/tables/state`, outbox, PWA, minimal shift open, heartbeat, enrolment. Move to Phase 2: Premjesti sto, Predaj sto kolegi, Bez stola, *Nedavno* tab, card tips. Delete the 10 s *Poništi* entirely: after *Potvrdi* the toast says 'Poslano · Sto 7 · Tura 1' and a mistaken lock is fixed by the 120 s self-void on S2 (same logged `line_adjustments` row, `reason='wrong_entry'`), which also removes the awkward case of undoing an order that is still in the outbox. Re-estimate Phase 1 at 50–70 h, Phase 2 at 45–60 h, Phase 3 at 40–55 h (≈ 150–210 h to end of Phase 3, 11–14 weeks at 15 h/week) and state the pilot start as 'after Phase 2, whenever that lands' rather than week 8.
- **[major] Stale `devices.pending_count` blocks a waiter's settlement forever when his phone dies** — F10 step 2: settlement refused with `409 PENDING_OUTBOX` while the device heartbeat reports `pending_count > 0`. `pending_count` is a value pushed by the phone every 60 s; a phone that ran out of battery, was dropped, or simply lost data reception at 01:40 with 2 queued rounds leaves `pending_count=2` on the server indefinitely — there is no heartbeat that would reset it. The waiter cannot settle that night nor any following night without an approver override in `audit_log`, and if the phone is replaced the old device row still says 2. The plan's own stance is 'treat the outbox as volatile; the shift reconciliation, not the phone, is the accountability mechanism', which this rule contradicts.
  - Fix: (1) Block only when the heartbeat is fresh: `pending_count > 0 AND last_seen_at > now − 10 min` → `409 PENDING_OUTBOX` with 'Amarov telefon: 2 neposlane, javio se prije 3 min'. (2) Stale device (`last_seen_at` older than 10 min or `pending_count > 0` for > 30 min) → settlement allowed, `waiter_settlements.unsent_at_settle = pending_count`, the shift lands in *Zahtijeva pažnju* as 'Amar: 2 ture nisu stigle sa telefona', and the rounds are back-filled by the owner as `source='manual'` or arrive later under the late-sync rule; the settlement is not blocked but the flag stays on the row so the diff is read with that in mind. (3) Client clears `pending_count` on the server with the last successful flush, and the heartbeat payload includes `oldest_pending_at` so the owner sees how long the round has been stuck. (4) `POST /api/devices/heartbeat` also runs on every successful flush, not only on the 60 s timer.
- **[major] drizzle-kit does not manage triggers, and SQLite table recreation in later migrations silently drops them** — §4 and Phase 0 put the append-only and `line_adjustments` transition triggers 'in the first migration'. drizzle-kit has no trigger support in the schema (assumption, not verified against the current release, but consistent with every drizzle release to date): triggers must be hand-written into a custom migration file. More dangerous: for SQLite, drizzle-kit implements many schema changes (dropping a column, changing NOT NULL, renaming, adding a FK) by creating `__new_orders`, copying rows and renaming — a trigger attached to the old `orders` table is gone after that migration, with no error. A Phase 2 change such as adding `decided_in_shift_id` with a foreign key, or tightening a column, would remove the append-only guarantee and nothing in the deploy would notice until the nightly recompute happens to disagree.
  - Fix: Keep triggers out of drizzle's hands: `server/db/triggers.sql` holds every trigger as `DROP TRIGGER IF EXISTS x; CREATE TRIGGER x ...` and a small `applyTriggers(db)` runs it at process start after `migrate()` (idempotent, ~50 ms). The Phase 0 vitest (a) 'UPDATE/DELETE on every ledger table throws' runs against a DB built by `migrate()` + `applyTriggers()` and enumerates the ledger tables from a constant in `shared/constants.ts` so a new ledger table without a trigger fails the test. Add a test that introspects `sqlite_master` for every trigger name in the constant list. Note in README: 'never `drizzle-kit push`; after `generate`, open the SQL and check for `__new_` tables — if present, the triggers are re-applied at boot, but foreign keys and indexes must be checked by hand'.
- **[major] Business-date, DST and device-clock rules are underspecified and will be implemented from `process.env.TZ`** — §3 F1: `business_date` = 'local time minus business_day_start_hour'; §5 sets `Environment=TZ=Europe/Sarajevo` on the systemd unit; timestamps are UTC ISO. An AI coder will compute business_date with `new Date().getHours()`, which is correct on the VPS and wrong in vitest on Vedran's Mac, wrong for a second venue in another zone, and fragile on the DST nights (2026-03-29 02:00→03:00, 2026-10-25 03:00→02:00 — both during open hours for a lounge). The late-sync rule compares `client_created_at` (phone clock) with `tabs.closed_at` (server clock); a phone with manual time 40 min off classifies fresh rounds as late or hides genuinely late ones, and `sync_lag_s` becomes noise. Nothing in the plan records device clock skew.
  - Fix: (1) `venues.settings_json.timezone = 'Europe/Sarajevo'`; `shared/dates.ts` exports `businessDate(utcIso, tz, startHour)` implemented with `Intl.DateTimeFormat(..., {timeZone: tz, hour12: false})` parts (no library needed) and unit tests for 05:59 vs 06:00 local, 2026-10-25 02:30 local (ambiguous hour) and 2026-03-29 02:30 (non-existent hour). The systemd `TZ` stays for logs only; no business code may call `getHours()` without a zone — add an eslint `no-restricted-syntax` rule or a grep in CI. (2) `POST /api/devices/heartbeat` sends `client_now`; server stores `devices.clock_skew_s = server_now − client_now`; every outbox payload carries `client_created_at` and the server stores `client_created_at_adj = client_created_at + skew` and uses the adjusted value for the late-sync test and `sync_lag_s`; `|skew| > 300` is a *Zahtijeva pažnju* line 'Amarov telefon: sat kasni 12 min'. (3) `shifts.business_date` is computed from `opened_at` only, once, and never re-derived.
- **[major] Blind cash handover is defeated by the waiter's own screens** — §7 rule 7 and F10 promise that `expected_fen` is revealed only after the waiter declares. S1 keeps per-table totals for open tables (needed to serve), which is a soft leak the owner is told about on *Pravila*: the design catches inattention and rounding theft, not a waiter with a calculator — the spot count and the coal-per-bowl proxy remain the hard checks. Add this to the Phase 2 done-criteria: 'before settlement `/api/me/shift` contains no `*_fen` field'.
- **[major] Waste below the PIN threshold is unlimited per shift — a 9,99 KM-at-a-time write-off channel** — §2 and rule 9: waiters log waste (razbijeno/prosuto) without approval below `waste_pin_threshold_fen` (10 KM); waste reduces expected stock (§8: expected = … − waste) so it hides a taken bottle from the spot count. A waiter can log 'prosuto 1 × Coca-Cola' five times a night at 2 KM each, or 'razbijeno 1 × Jägermeister 0.7' at 9 KM, and the spot count balances. §7's table reports storno %, comp %, cash diff and manjak but has no waste-per-person metric; the Telegram rules include `stock_variance` but not waste.
  - Fix: (1) Add `waste_shift_fen` (default 2000 = 20 KM) to `settings_json`: Σ own waste in this shift beyond it requires an approver PIN regardless of the single-event threshold. (2) Waste of any `is_spot` item always requires an approver PIN (these are exactly the theft targets). (3) Add the row 'Otpis' to the reported-metrics table: waste value per waiter per shift; > 10 KM or > 3 events per shift → 'označeno za razgovor', shown on S11 and the per-waiter strip. (4) `waste_events` counts as a *Zahtijeva pažnju* line when the same item is written off on 3 shifts in 7 days. All four are a few lines of SQL in `services/stock.ts` and a settings default; no new tables.
- **[major] Every stock item starts with `unit_cost_fen = 0`, so 'manjak u KM' and the 10 KM variance alert are silent for the whole pilot** — §8: `avg_cost_fen`/`last_cost_fen` are updated only by deliveries; `opening` movements from *Početno stanje* carry no cost; spot-count `variance_fen = variance_qty × unit_cost_fen`.   - Fix: (1) The BiH lounge template and the *Početno stanje* screen carry a mandatory `last_cost_fen` per stock item (owner types the purchase price per pack; the screen converts to base unit), written into `stock_items.avg_cost_fen` and the `opening` movements' `unit_cost_fen`. (3) Phase 3 test: a spot count on an item with only an `opening` movement (cost 180) reports −1,80 KM.
- **[major] Payment and expected-cash rules are inconsistent between venue and per-waiter views and leave overpayment, tips and reversal method undefined** — F10 step 6 defines venue `cash_expected = opening_float + Σ cash payments − Σ payouts + Σ float_in`, but the per-waiter formula adds 'totals of own unpaid tabs still pending_review' — so Σ per-waiter expected ≠ venue expected and the shift-level diff will not equal the sum of settlement diffs, which the owner will notice on the cash-box panel the first night. Tips: `amount_fen` vs `received_fen` vs `tip_fen` on cash — whether the tip is drawer money or the waiter's is not stated, and with `track_cash_tips=false` 'Ostatak je napojnica' has no defined effect. Multi-payment: 'tab becomes paid when Σ payments ≥ tab total' allows `amount_fen` above the remaining balance (a double-submitted 50 KM on a 47,50 KM tab yields 100 KM of cash 'expected' that the waiter never received) and does not say what happens when a new round is locked after a partial payment. A cash reversal for a paid void on a mixed cash+card tab has no rule for which method reverses.
  - Fix: One function `expectedCash(tx, shiftId, userId?)` in `services/shifts.ts` used by both views, with the identity tested: `venueExpected = opening_float + Σ float_in − Σ payouts + Σ_over_waiters(waiterExpected)`. Define: waiter expected = Σ own cash `payments.amount_fen` (reversals included, `shift_id` = this shift) + Σ totals of own tabs with `status='unpaid' AND pending_review=1`; venue expected includes the same pending-unpaid term (so the formula in F10 step 6 gains '+ Σ pending unpaid tabs'). Tips: `amount_fen` is always the bill portion and is the only value entering expected cash; `tip_fen` is the waiter's, recorded only for reporting when `track_cash_tips`; `received_fen` is display only (kusur). Server rule in `POST /api/payments`: `amount_fen ≤ remaining_fen` else `422 OVERPAY`; `tabs.status='paid'` is set inside that transaction when remaining reaches 0; a lock on a `paid` tab creates a new tab (second bill), never reopens. Reversal method: cash if the tab has any cash payment, else card (no cash effect); state this in F6 step 4.

On iOS the home-screen web app runs with its own cookie jar and IndexedDB, isolated from Safari (assumption from documented WebKit behaviour since iOS 16.4-era; verify on one iPhone in Phase 1). The installed app therefore has no `sank_d` cookie, the single-use 6-char code is already `used_at`, and the waiter is stuck at 'Uređaj nije prijavljen' — the owner reads him a second code and now the same person has two device rows, which §7's 'same user on two devices' rule then flags. Feedback on lock: use a visual flash + the haptic only where `navigator.vibrate` exists (`'vibrate' in navigator`). Replace 'home-screen apps are assumed exempt from the 7-day cap' with the outcome the plan already chose: the outbox is volatile, the 5-minute unsent banner and the heartbeat make it visible.
- **[minor] 'važi od: sljedeće smjene' contradicts 'price copied from products now' at lock** — §9 page 4 offers a scheduled price change writing `price_history.valid_from` for the next shift, while F3 step 3 snapshots `unit_price_fen` 'from `products` now' and §7 rule 2 repeats it. Either the lock must read `price_history` (effective row where `valid_from ≤ now`), or something must copy the scheduled price into `products.price_fen` when the next shift opens — neither is specified, and an AI coder will implement the display side and forget the lock side, producing rounds locked at the old price after the owner believes the price changed.
  - Fix: Drop scheduling in v1: a price edit applies immediately, writes `price_history(valid_from = now, valid_to = NULL on the new row, valid_to = now on the previous)` and `audit_log`, and the menu page says 'Promjena važi odmah — mijenjaj cijene prije otvaranja smjene'. The lock keeps copying `products.price_fen`. Add to Phase 0 test (d) that `price_history` has exactly one row per product with `valid_to IS NULL`. Revisit scheduling in Phase 5 if the owner asks.
- **[minor] `stock_movements.reverses_id` and the flavour `in_stock` toggle reference structures that do not fit recipes** — F6 step 4 and §8: an applied void 'writes `sale_storno` with `reverses_id`'. A line with a recipe (mix shisha: 10 g + 10 g + 3 kom coal) produced three `sale` movements; a partial void (qty 1 of 3 espressos) reverses a fraction of one. A single `reverses_id` on a storno movement is ambiguous and a coder will pick the first sale row. §9 page 4 has a flavour `in_stock` toggle and S4 greys flavours 'Nema'; flavours are `stock_items` with `category='duhan'` and no such column exists, and on-hand for open tins (quarter-tin estimates) will hover near zero, wrongly greying flavours.
  - Fix: Storno movements: `ref_type='line_adjustment', ref_id=adjustment.id`, one row per recipe component with `qty_delta = +component_qty × adjustment.qty`, `unit_cost_fen` copied from the matching `sale` row for the same `stock_item_id` and `order_line_id`; drop `reverses_id` from `stock_movements` (keep it on `payments`). Flavour availability: add `stock_items.available INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 1` toggled by the owner on the flavours page; S4 greys on `available=0` only, and shows a small 'malo' hint when on-hand < one tin. Phase 3 test: a void of 1 of 2 mix shishas writes exactly three storno rows (+10 g, +10 g, +3 kom).
- **[minor] 'closed shifts annotate-only' and 'status columns recomputed server-side' are promised as trigger-enforced but no trigger is specified** — §2: 'change a closed shift except by annotating it. Database triggers enforce this, not only code.' §4 lists append-only triggers and the `line_adjustments` transition trigger, but `shifts`, `tabs` and `stock_counts` are mutable tables with status machines and no trigger text. Opus will implement the guard in the service layer only, and an admin CRUD route or a future migration script can then reopen a closed shift or flip a `tabs.status` from `paid` to `open`.

Add these three to Phase 0 test (a).
- **[minor] No-šanker nights: who accepts the closer's own cash and signs the settlement is undefined** — `waiter_settlements.accepted_by` is NOT NULL and F10 requires a 'receiver PIN'; F1/§2 allow a waiter to open and, by implication, close a shift when nobody is behind the bar (open question 2 acknowledges the scenario but the schema does not). At 02:00 a lone waiter closing the shift would have to accept his own handover with his own PIN, which an AI coder will either forbid (shift cannot close → next day's open blocked by the partial unique index) or allow silently (self-accepted settlement looks like any other).
  - Fix: Allow self-acceptance explicitly: `accepted_by = user_id` permitted only when no other approver is a `shift_members` row still present, set `waiter_settlements.self_accepted=1`, and list the shift in *Zahtijeva pažnju* as 'Amar sam predao 312,50 KM — provjeri kovertu'; the owner enters `counted_fen` next morning via a `cash_movements(type='owner_count')`-style row (or a `shifts.owner_counted_fen` column) and the diff shown thereafter is owner-counted vs expected. The same rule covers the `pooled` case with a lone closer.

### completeness

The plan is unusually strong on the ledger and accountability core, but three defects would stop the pilot as written: offline payments and tab actions cannot address a server-created tab, the append-only settlement and summary tables cannot absorb the post-close approvals the plan itself promises, and Phase 1 can open shifts it cannot close. A further cluster of contradictions sits at the seams between sections (void reasons vs restock, price scheduling vs price snapshot, duplicate payments, per-waiter floats, forgotten closes, opening stock without cost), each fixable with one column or one rule. The softer gaps are real too: no owner-side continuity when Vedran is unavailable, no pilot metrics or baseline, nothing staff gain for themselves, and no data-protection notice now that the BiH law is in force. None of the fixes change the stack or the phase order; most belong in Phase 0's schema and tests.

- **[critical] Offline payment, unpaid, move and assign cannot reference a tab the server has not created yet** — §6 says 'the server owns tab creation' and §3 F3 creates the `tabs` row inside `POST /api/orders`. §3 F5 then queues `{client_id, tab_id, method, ...}` to `POST /api/tabs/:id/pay`, and `/unpaid`, `/move`, `/assign` all take `:id`. With Wi-Fi off, a waiter who locks a first round on an empty table and then collects cash has no server `tab_id` on the phone; the payment outbox entry cannot be built, and the Phase 1 done-criterion ('two rounds and a payment queued offline appear exactly once') is unachievable as specified. The `late_sync` rule (`client_created_at < tabs.closed_at`) has the same hole: an offline second round has no `tab_id` to compare against.
  - Fix: Add `tabs.client_id TEXT NOT NULL` with `UNIQUE(venue_id, client_id)`. The phone mints a `tab_client_id` (uuid) the first time it opens S2 for a table with no known open tab, stores it in the draft (`draft:{user_id}:{table_id}`), and sends it on every outbox entry: `orders {client_id, tab_client_id, table_id, ...}`, `pay {client_id, tab_client_id, ...}`. Server find-or-create for a tab: by `(venue_id, tab_client_id)` first, else by open tab on `(shift, table_id)` (in which case the phone's `tab_client_id` is recorded in `order_events` and the response returns the canonical `tabs.id`+`client_id` so the phone adopts it). Routes become `POST /api/tabs/pay`, `/unpaid`, `/move`, `/assign` with `tab_client_id` in the body (or accept either id in `:id`). Two phones on table 5 both send their own `tab_client_id`; the server resolves both to the single open tab and answers with the canonical id, so the 'two waiters on one table' case stays correct. Add to Phase 0 tests: pay queued before the first lock synced resolves to the same tab.
- **[critical] Append-only `waiter_settlements` and `shift_summaries` contradict 'approval recomputes expected cash'** — §4 lists `waiter_settlements` and `shift_summaries` under append-only triggers. §3 F6 step 5 says owner approval of a pending void 'recomputes the shift's expected cash and stores the old value in audit_log', §3 F10 stores `expected_fen`/`diff_fen` in `waiter_settlements`, and §7 reads `Razlika pazara` from `waiter_settlements.diff_fen` (red after 3 flagged shifts in 30 days). After a legitimate morning approval the stored `diff_fen` is frozen wrong forever (the waiter keeps a flagged shift he was later cleared of, exactly the unfairness the plan promises to avoid), and the 05:30 nightly recompute will report 'drift' against `shift_summaries` every night that contains a post-close decision, training the owner to ignore the integrity alarm.
  - Fix: Store only what a human entered in `waiter_settlements`: `declared_fen`, `expected_at_declare_fen`, `accepted_by`, `created_at`; derive `diff_fen` at read time as `declared − expected(now)` where expected excludes voids `applied` after `created_at` only if `reverses` money was actually returned (it wasn't — pending voids leave the money with the waiter, so once applied, the cash physically handed over is unchanged; expected must subtract the voided amount). Make `shift_summaries` a snapshot ledger: PK `(shift_id, version)`, `computed_at`, `trigger ∈ close|decision|nightly`; the close writes version 1, every `line_adjustments` decision on a closed shift writes a new version in the same transaction, and the nightly task compares its recompute to the latest version — drift means a code bug, not a decision. `Razlika pazara` and the per-waiter strip read the latest derived diff; the *Smjena* page shows 'prvobitno −12,00 · nakon odobrenja −2,00'.
- **[critical] No runbook and no ownership plan for the week Vedran is unavailable** — §12 risk 6 covers VPS death with backups, but nothing covers the case the brief explicitly raises: the app is down or broken and the only developer (a friend, unpaid, sharing his own snajper VPS and domain) is on holiday or has fallen out with the owner. The plan puts the lounge's only sales/stock record on Vedran's box under `sank.<domain>` — the owner cannot even restart the service. There is no uptime check, `Restart=` policy, monitoring of the backup task, or written fallback for staff.
  - Fix: Phase 0 additions: (1) VPS and domain either registered/paid in the owner's name with Vedran as admin, or a written agreement that the DB file and backups are the owner's and will be handed over on request; (2) `sank.service` with `Restart=always`, `RestartSec=5`; (3) a free external ping (UptimeRobot on `GET /api/health` every 5 min, and healthchecks.io pinged by the backup and nightly tasks) alerting the owner's Telegram directly, not only Vedran; (4) `deploy/OWNER_RUNBOOK.md` in Bosnian, one page: 'aplikacija ne radi' → paper blok + this photo → back-fill as `source='manual'` next day; how to download last night's backup from R2 (link in the runbook); Vedran's and one backup contact's numbers; (5) a second person (any developer friend, or the owner himself following the runbook) holds SSH access and the R2 credentials in a shared password-manager vault. Pilot exit criterion: the owner restarts the service and restores yesterday's backup once, from the runbook, without Vedran on the phone.
- **[major] Phase 1 opens shifts but cannot close them, so day 2 of the shadow-run is blocked** — §11 Phase 1 ships 'minimal F1 (*Otvori smjenu*)' and the partial unique index 'one open or closing shift per venue' (§4), but F10 close is Phase 2. On the second shadow-run night nobody can open a shift; the 'two waiters shadow-run a Friday' criterion works once and then the app is stuck, and orders for the next night would attach to the wrong `shift_id`/`business_date`.
  - Fix: Add to Phase 1 a minimal `POST /api/shifts/:id/close` — closer PIN, no settlements, no count — writing `status='closed'`, `closing_note='zatvoreno bez predaje (faza 1)'` and an `audit_log` row; the same endpoint later becomes the owner's remote/forced close (see the 'forgotten close' issue). Also decide what an order does when no shift is open: currently `shift_id` would be null; rule: `POST /api/orders` returns `409 NO_OPEN_SHIFT` and the waiter sees the §3 F1 empty state, or auto-opens a shift when `waiter_can_open_shift` is on.
- **[major] Every applied void restocks, including voids for drinks that were consumed; comps after payment never refund** — §3 F6 step 4: `applied` always writes `sale_storno` movements. Reason chips include `complaint` and `walked_out` — the drink was served and drunk; re-adding it to stock creates a phantom surplus that the blind spot count then 'catches' as a manjak against whoever counts. §3 F7 itself states this danger for comps and solves it there, but F6 ignores it. Conversely, a post-lock `kind='comp'` on a line that is already paid (guest complains after paying) reduces the tab total but writes no reversing payment, so the waiter's expected cash stays higher than what he should now hold, or the guest gets no money back.
  - Fix: Add `line_adjustments.restock INTEGER NOT NULL` set server-side from reason: `wrong_entry`, `not_served`, `guest_changed_mind` → 1; `complaint`, `walked_out`, `other` → 0 (approver may flip it on the sheet: 'Piće vraćeno u šank?'). Only `restock=1` writes `sale_storno`. Money side is independent: if `was_paid=1`, both voids and comps write the reversing `payments` row (`amount_fen<0`, `reverses_id`, `method` = original) in the same transaction; a card reversal shows 'Vrati na terminalu — unesi ref' and lands in *Zahtijeva pažnju*. Rename the walked-out chip: `walked_out` belongs to *Nije plaćeno* (F5), remove it from the void chips to stop the double path.
- **[major] Price scheduling 'važi od sljedeće smjene' contradicts 'price copied from `products` now'; silent total drift on the phone** — §9 page 4 writes `price_history.valid_from` for 'sljedeće smjene', but §3 F3 snapshots `unit_price_fen` 'from `products` now'. Either the future price is written into `products.price_fen` immediately (waiters charge it tonight) or it is never applied. Separately, the lock sheet total is computed from the phone's cached menu; if the server price differs the waiter has already told the guest 8,50 KM and the tab is 9,00 KM, with no signal.
  - Fix: Make `price_history` the source at lock: `SELECT price_fen FROM price_history WHERE product_id=?
